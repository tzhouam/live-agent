1. **Major — end evidence becomes stale after a backward move** (`renderFrame`, `src.onended`). A chunk can send `chunk_end` with `painted` and `tp_last_paint`, then remain drawable for 650 ms. If the viewer moves the slider back, later paints update those fields locally but never reach the server. I agree that the reported painted set is conservative for QC; I do **not** agree that its `E` can still be called the *last* painted frame or time. Keep the playback acknowledgment, then send final evidence when the chunk leaves retention, or explicitly treat the first report as a snapshot and its `E` as a lower bound.

2. **Major — the fallback can wait forever while rendering fails** (`render`, `renderFrame`, `src.onended`). `lastRaf` is refreshed before rendering completes. If `renderFrame` repeatedly throws while the tab stays visible, the catch keeps the animation loop alive, and `onended` sees a recent `lastRaf` on every check. No `chunk_end` is sent. Track successful rendering separately, or ensure a render error cannot prevent the clock based end acknowledgment.

3. **Minor — the page check does not establish two claimed cases** (`offset_change`). It checks captions but never checks the displayed board. It also does not require a slider move to reenter a chunk that has already sent `chunk_end`; a move wholly inside the current chunk can pass the gap check. Make that boundary deterministic and assert the previous chunk’s key, caption, and board after the move.
