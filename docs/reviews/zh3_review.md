### Findings

1. **[blocking; carried over] The latency model predicts presentation before its available slot.** Under §5.4’s L=0 rule, \(P_{k+1}=V_k\). Even with zero teacher and generation time, request \(k+1\) cannot be shown before \(V_k+R\); its student also depends on \(k\)’s completed KV. Thus the ideal median is at least **7.5 s** from a uniformly timed instruction before adding \(A\) or \(I\), not §6.4’s 2.5 s. With the section’s illustrative \(A=I=0.5\) s, the paced median is at least **8.5 s**, not 6.5 s; L=1 adds another 5 s. This undermines the TVC and TFMR interpretation in §8 and contradicts D-018’s amended just-in-time lock. **Settling check:** define lock, previous-request completion, first-chunk readiness and presentation timestamps on one timeline; recalculate targets from that model and M0 traces.

2. **[blocking; carried over] The proposed forward correction has no valid deadline model.** Under the proposal’s own L=0 gate, \(k+1\) is locked at \(V_k\), so a VLM judgment of *displayed* content from \(k\) cannot enter \(k+1\). A late block of \(k\) may also miss \(k+2\), especially once VLM judgment, prompt revision, compilation, moderation and the next audio-teacher start are included. The §5.4 \(k+1\)-or-\(k+2\) rule therefore overstates repair eligibility and invalidates RQ2’s “earliest” request as written. **Settling check:** measure the complete correction chain \(C\) and assign each failure to the first request \(j\) whose prompt has not locked at `visible_time + C`; report missed and ineligible repairs.

3. **[blocking; new] M0 cannot perform the qualification it is asked to perform.** §§6.4 and 7.0 call for end-to-end teacher/student overlap, encoding, presentation and \(\Lambda\), but T2’s resident incremental teacher and T3’s chunk publication are scheduled for **M1**. The released runtime prepares all audio first and writes one MP4 at the end, as the inlined `cli.py:219–229` and `runner.py:293–330` show. §1.2 also says the first demo needs a configuration that passed “real-time qualification” in M0, whereas §6.4 requires an M1 soak test. The published first-playable figure is from a separate benchmark and excludes internal audio preparation. [TaoMate-H3 README](https://github.com/TaoLiveAIGC/TaoMate-H3) **Settling check:** make M0 a preliminary component profile, move end-to-end qualification after T1–T3, and measure the actual client playback path before applying the §8 targets.

4. **[major; carried over] The compiler and speech acceptance path remain underspecified for H3.** H3 takes **one string per five-second request**; the six-field `action/transcript/subject/scene/camera/voice` convention described in §5.2 comes from TaoMate-LTX. H3’s released T2AV path has no voice reference, and its separate base-H3 teacher supplies the final audio latents, as the inlined `runtime.py:339–359,739–743` shows. A text voice description and intended transcript do not establish voice identity or exact spoken content. Pre-generation script moderation cannot catch an off-script utterance; §10’s post-generation ASR sample cannot prevent one from being presented. **Settling check:** show an actual BlockScript-to-H3 prompt, calibrate spoken-word and speaker fidelity in E1, and specify a timed generated-audio check or an explicit limit on what can be guaranteed before playback.

5. **[major; carried over] RQ4 and RQ5 still lack comparisons that identify their claimed mechanisms.** RQ4’s arms change ledger use, textual correction and resets together; RQ5 claims lower false-user-fact rate without naming a matched untyped-memory comparator. Human adjudication and clustering are improvements, but they do not remove these confounds. The exact user-level leakage bound with 300 independent users is mathematically plausible only if zero leaks are **observed across the claimed channel**; ASR plus sampled listening does not establish that for generated audio. **Settling check:** use the logged matched factorial RQ4 arms, a retrieval-budget-matched RQ5 control, and full listening of the speech leak probes.

6. **[major; carried over] The Co-Director adaptation is both misdescribed and unevaluable.** The paper selects a combination from \(3\times3\times4\) possibilities but uses **three factor-specific rewards to update selected axis values independently**, rather than treating every combination as one undifferentiated arm. [Co-Director paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.24842) The proposal does not say which style choices may vary without changing canonical appearance or voice. Updating the bandit across evaluation sessions would also change A2 during a paired comparison. **Settling check:** constrain arms to canon-safe choices, state the per-axis updates, train on separate pilot sessions, freeze the policy for the main comparison and test its incremental effect separately.

7. **[major; new] The system-level baseline no longer tests the backbone decision.** B0 is a noninteractive fixed loop; A1 and A2 both use H3. That isolates the online director for RQ1, but it cannot answer whether the resulting persona is more responsive or useful than the simple **interactive LLM + TTS + talking-head** baseline retained in D-004. This matters when speech cannot be streamed independently of H3’s five-second audio teacher. The clip library supplies visual acknowledgement only; it cannot meet TFMR. **Settling check:** retain an interactive speech/avatar comparator for end-to-end latency, speech, preference and cost, while keeping A1–A2 as the controlled director comparison.

8. **[major; new] The “near-real-time” fallback needs a stable service rule.** §1.2 says to queue instructions for the next five-second request if real-time qualification fails. On the reported 8×H20 setup, DiT alone takes 14.81 s for 10 s of output, about **7.4 s per five-second request**, before teacher, decoding or transport; a continuing five-second arrival cadence therefore grows a queue. [TaoMate-H3 README](https://github.com/TaoLiveAIGC/TaoMate-H3) Reaction clips can mask a wait briefly but do not bound it. **Settling check:** define admission control, skipped requests, slower playback or a turn-based mode, and measure wait growth and TFMR under sustained interaction.

9. **[minor; carried over] Tighten evidence labels and source scope.** The five-second/four-chunk contract, sink-plus-two-recent KV, teacher overwrite, shipped FA3 requirement and upstream 8×H20 timings are supported by the inlined evidence. The \(0.68\times\) ratio is **【推导】** from upstream DiT time, not itself 【引用】; KV is not the generator’s only state; and VQQA’s generator is not API-only. The 17.287 s “first playable” value should continue to be identified as a benchmark outside the released one-shot output path. [TaoMate-H3 README](https://github.com/TaoLiveAIGC/TaoMate-H3), [VQQA paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.12310)

The Google-suite ideas are reasonable research inspirations, and the proposal now correctly avoids claiming image re-anchoring for released T2AV. The **online** benefits remain hypotheses. A corrected request timeline, an executable speech and publication path, and the matched comparisons above are needed before this version is ready.
