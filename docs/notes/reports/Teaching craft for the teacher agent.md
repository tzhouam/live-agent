# Teaching craft distils into one checkable loop

Chinese teacher-education textbooks, the international evidence-based texts and the documented practice of award-winning teachers on both sides prescribe the same lesson loop, and it is specific enough to script for a one-to-one video teacher with a text board: write the 课题 and one to three goals on the board in the first minute; let the student attempt a 尝试题 before any explanation; teach only what the attempt exposed, in small steps, each with a worked model and a question the student must answer; practise and correct inside the lesson; and have the student, not the teacher, say what was learned. The Chinese side supplies the hard time rules and the assessed artefacts — teacher talk **no more than 10 minutes** of 45 (邱学华), the **last 15 minutes** reserved for independent work (洋思), **6-8-minute** segments (杜郎口), 板书 worth **8 of the 35** 教学实施 points in the 教师资格证 interview, and competition rubrics that put **70-80 %** of the score on the live lesson. The international side supplies the dials: a **5-8-minute** opening review, **about 80 %** success during practice, a **3-second** minimum wait after every question, hinge questions answerable in 2 minutes and read in 30 seconds, comment-only feedback that leaves the student something to do, and a veto on person-praise (effect size **0.14**). China's 特级教师, 全国教书育人楷模 and 国家级教学成果奖 winners converge on nine imitable moves (a conflict-laden 情境导入, pupil-first attempt, one big question per block, error-as-material, 变式 and 一题多解, a structured board, reading aloud, specific praise, an in-lesson check), while international prize citations mostly document access and belonging and the mechanics come from Shanghai, Japanese lesson study, Direct Instruction and Lemov. Grade band changes tone, pace and abstraction — objects and echoing for grades 1-2, cognitive conflict and peer-praise for 3-6, timed silent 自学 and 商量 for 7-9, conjecture-then-prove and dry puzzles for 10-12 — but not the loop. Two of these numbers (success rate and wait time) can be measured from the first pilot; the rest are cited prescriptions with uneven evidence, and the report flags where the evidence is contested or the source unverified. Every figure below is *cited* from the linked source unless marked *estimate* or *proposed target*; nothing was measured locally.

## Chinese textbooks fix the skeleton and cap teacher talk at ten minutes

The canon is narrow. The national 教育硕士 333《教育综合》syllabus names 王道俊、郭文安《教育学》(人民教育出版社, 2009) and 陈琦、刘儒德《教育心理学》(高等教育出版社) as its reference books ([333 考试大纲](http://cdn1.kybimg.com/ohr/2019/07/13/094104_5d2936b01d8fa.pdf)), and 教师资格证 candidates study exam-prep condensations keyed to the 教育部 2011《中小学和幼儿园教师资格考试标准（试行）》, which defines 教学知识与能力 as 学科知识、教学设计、教学实施、教学评价 for each of 幼儿园/小学/初中/高中 ([2011 考试标准](https://www.wic.edu.cn/upload/20260521/9d99b67b3edce71df4045951441c7d30.pdf)). The most useful structural text for an agent is 崔允漷's《有效教学》(华东师范大学出版社, 2009), whose table of contents sorts teacher behaviour into 主要教学行为 (呈现、对话、指导) and 辅助教学行为 (动机、强化、教师期望、课堂气氛) ([豆瓣 TOC](https://book.douban.com/subject/3854719/)) — a ready-made action space of explain, question and guide, plus motivate and reinforce.

What these books prescribe for a lesson is a fixed sequence with a fixed checklist. The 教学工作基本环节 are 备课、上课、课外作业的布置与批改、课外辅导、学业成绩的检查和评定, with 上课 as "教学工作的中心环节" and 备课 as 三备 (备教材、备学生、备教法); a good lesson satisfies eight criteria — **目的明确、内容正确、方法得当、结构合理、语言艺术、气氛热烈、板书有序、态度从容** — and its most fundamental requirement is 充分发挥学生主体性 ([华图 教学过程的基本环节](https://m.jl.huatu.com/2021/0614/1705933.html)). Inside the lesson the exam-prep 教案 skeleton runs 教学目标 (三维: 知识与能力、过程与方法、情感态度价值观, "明确、具体、简练") → 教学重难点 (难点 = "学生难于理解、掌握或容易引起混淆、错误的内容") → 导入新课 → 新课讲授 → 课堂提问及练习 → 课堂总结 → 板书设计, and the 总结 is typed as 封闭型 (refocus attention on the key points) or 开放型 (extend, or link into the knowledge system) ([233 考点 PDF](https://img3.233.com/2019-07/26/156412076595801.pdf)). The 2011 standard states the same loop as competencies: create a 教学情景 that "有效地将学生引入学习活动", guide learning so as to "发挥学生学习的主体性和积极性", interact, and close by "归纳、提炼" and "合理布置作业"; for 初中 it adds "条理清楚、重点突出", and for 初中/高中 "组织探究性教学与研究性学习" ([2011 考试标准](https://www.wic.edu.cn/upload/20260521/9d99b67b3edce71df4045951441c7d30.pdf)). A university 教案 regulation gives the minimum: whatever the format, an 教案 "都必须具备三个基本要素即：教学内容、教学目的、教学过程", the goal must be "适度" (了解、理解、掌握、灵活运用), methods are chosen "紧紧围绕教学重点、难点", and the 课后小结 is the teacher's "课后的反省薄" ([河北传媒学院 教案编写规范](https://www.hebic.cn/uploadfile/2020/1026/20201026104254641.pdf)).

The eight 教学原则 in the syllabus — 科学性与思想性统一、理论联系实际、直观性、启发性、循序渐进、巩固性、发展性/量力性、因材施教 — each carry 贯彻要求 that read as rules: 直观 "要与讲解相结合"; 启发 "启发学生独立思考…让学生动手"; 巩固 "在理解的基础上巩固…在扩充改组和运用知识中积极巩固"; 循序渐进 "由浅入深，由易到难，由简到繁"; 因材施教 "针对学生的特点进行有区别的教学" ([233 考点 PDF](https://img3.233.com/2019-07/26/156412076595801.pdf)). Their ancestor is《学记》, taught verbatim: "道而弗牽，強而弗抑，開而弗達" (guide, do not drag; open a path, do not deliver the conclusion), "教也者，長善而救其失者也", and "善待問者，如撞鐘，叩之以小者則小鳴，叩之以大者則大鳴" ([《禮記·學記》](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/%E7%A6%AE%E8%A8%98/%E5%AD%B8%E8%A8%98)). Teacher education reads 开而弗达 as "an explanation ends in a question the student can answer with a 跳一跳, not in the conclusion", and 长善救失 as differentiated teaching from each learner's strengths and typical faults ([常国良 on 学记](https://www.rujiazg.com/article/22532)); 撞钟 is the textbook licence for adaptive answer length — a short answer to a small question, a full one to a big question.

Microteaching (微格教学) courses train 导入、讲解、提问、板书、结束、变化 as separately drilled skills ([上海建桥学院 微格教学训练大纲](https://wygj.gench.edu.cn/_upload/article/files/43/51/e5ae217147f383c9d79077f93a2b/1ee47c42-dad1-4c0f-a9ec-e788c7a23d4b.docx)), and the most operational rules are for questioning and the board. A teacher-training questioning handout fixes five 环节 (问题设计、提问、候答、叫答、理答), a wait of **3-5 seconds** after asking (**10-15 seconds** when several answers are possible) and again before the teacher responds, **2-3 core questions per lesson** placed at the 重点、难点 and 新旧知识衔接处, five 助答 moves in order (重复发问/申明题意 → 分解难点 → 转换角度 → 适当提示 → 补充修正), 追问 "在教学重难点特别是易错点处", 理答 by 澄清、追问、转问、悬置, question chains that start with "small easy questions first, each a prerequisite for the next", and feedback whose wording "要与学生的回答质量相一致" — specific, never "一棒子打死", with praise for a weak but serious answer ([课堂提问教学解读与评价](https://www.xyafu.edu.cn/__local/A/F4/5A/542638649C0D5860018A98BAA9E_788EA74A_338CA5.pdf)). Ten 导入 types are catalogued (直接、直观、故事、情景、温故、问题、实验、悬念、活动游戏、练习) under the maxim "好的导入是课堂教学成功的一半" ([十种导入法](https://www.hljp.edu.cn/kcszjxyj/info/1041/1574.htm)). 板书 is a required skill with types 提纲式、对比式、图表式、脉络式、总-分-总式、形象图解式 ([233 考点 PDF](https://img3.233.com/2019-07/26/156412076595801.pdf)), judged by 设计科学、言简意赅、条理性强、艺术性强 ([华立学院 评课诀窍](https://www.hualixy.edu.cn/jsfzzx/xxzy/content_66135)). The one opened pedagogical treatment adds the working conventions an agent's text board can follow directly: 正板书 (left or centre, "原则上讲…是不能擦掉的", holding 重难点 and 中心语句) versus 副板书 (the right edge, temporary, erased before the close); margins on all four sides; never "边讲边写边擦"; neither "课本的搬家" nor "教案的缩写"; and a finished board from which the student "应有窥斑见豹之感，能看出整堂课的知识框架" ([杨艳、杨六兰 2012](https://xbsk.xcc.edu.cn/xcxyxbsk/article/pdf/20120146)). 顾泠沅's 青浦 slides supply the canonical negative example for 讲解: a 正方形 lesson with **105 fill-in-the-blank exchanges, 74.3 % memory questions and 21 % simple reasoning**, "小步、多练、快进，未留思考空间给学生", defended by the teacher as "讲是给学生知识，问是看他们收到没有" ([顾泠沅 WALS 2007](https://eduhk.hk/wals/conference07/resources/wals07/2_29112007_gu.pdf)).

### Judges weight the live lesson at 70-80 % and score the board on its own

The rubrics teachers are judged by convert the textbook into weights, and they are the most portable Chinese artefact for the agent's self-check. Two 青教赛 feeder 评分表 that cite the 全国总工会 file chain split **教学设计 20 / 片段教学 70 / 教学反思 10** (华容县 2019, contestants prepare five 20-minute segments and draw one by lot before 12-15 live pupils) and **教学设计 20 / 教学展示 80** (岳阳市 2025: 20 minutes of 无生教学 plus 10 minutes of 说课, penalties from 10 seconds over time) ([华容县通知](https://huarong.gov.cn/uploadfiles/201903/20190313153918233.docx); [岳阳市方案](https://edu.yueyang.gov.cn/uploadfiles/202512/2025120109155930581.doc)). The biggest single rows are "突出重点、难点，讲授精当，富有启发性，能充分调动学生学习积极性" (20 of 70) and "主体意识明确，合作、探究，自主学习方式得到充分体现" (20 of 70), with 粉笔字与板书 a separate 5 of 70 split evenly between design ("构思精巧，条理清晰…能清晰地反映知识要点") and handwriting ("无错别字、漏字、多字，无繁体字、异体字") ([华容县通知](https://huarong.gov.cn/uploadfiles/201903/20190313153918233.docx)). School-level 优质课 sheets converge on **目标 9 / 内容 16 / 过程 50 / 基本功 15 / 效果 10**, with 8 points for "详略得当，突出重点、难点，易错点" and 8 for "多样化、个性化的评价方式激励学生" ([济源 2023 评价标准](https://jyj.jiyuan.gov.cn/14024/14027/17949/P020231030536421359312.doc)); the 中央电教馆 course-video standard tied to 一师一优课 uses 教学设计 15 / 教学过程 40 / 资源与技术 30 / 教师基本素质 15 and asks that "评价与激励穿插合理、简单有效" ([中教电〔2016〕35号](https://fz.sjtu.edu.cn/__local/3/36/71/8E261F07E250ED1650065D334EB_97D74B7F_D2A8B.pdf)). In the 教师资格证 interview (20 minutes preparation, then 5 minutes 结构化问答, 10 minutes 试讲, 5 minutes 答辩), 教学实施 carries **35 of 100** points: 6 for "情境创设合理，关注学习动机的激发", 10 for "教学内容表述和呈现清楚、准确", 4 for "提出的问题富有启发性", **8 for 板书** ("突出主题，层次分明；板书工整、美观、适量") and 7 for "教学环节安排合理；时间节奏控制恰当" ([中职 评分标准 repost](https://file.koolearn.com/20211211/16392008171758.doc); the 小学/中学 tables were only available as images, so their weights are not verified). The 小学 task card demands "配合教学内容适当板书、教学过程需有提问环节、试讲时要体现师生互动" ([小学面试大纲 repost](https://img3.233.com/2019-11/21/157430425098861.doc)), and 说课 delivered in place of 讲课 "不予给分" ([新疆 2025 面试公告](https://www.xjyt.gov.cn/d/file/p/2025/01-19/e76223e9958233fa3d24b978379bf4fa.pdf)). 说课 itself is the rationale layer — 说教材 → 说学情 → 说目标 → 说重难点及其确定的依据 → 说教法 → 说学法 → 说教学程序 with the time for each step → 说板书设计, in 15-20 minutes, every item answering "为什么这样教" ([陕西师大 说课简介](https://his.snnu.edu.cn/__local/C/88/76/867429B3AF5DD1B914C5E12036A_D35D8845_9400.doc)); its 评课 points make "能否体现学生的主体参与" the main criterion and require an "输出——反馈——调节——再输出" loop ([陕西师大 说课评课材料](https://newyjs.snnu.edu.cn/__local/4/C0/56/B72A430A93B0783AB77C1B8FC1B_09E184DB_F400.doc)). Timing guidance is thin but consistent: a 说课 should show how "一节课里的最佳时间（20——25分钟）和黄金时间（15分钟）" are used ([浙江科技学院 怎样说课](https://fdc.zust.edu.cn/__local/0/85/8A/7153CE7A32FFE6C2DAD7B6DEEC1_BD0A8BAF_6000.doc)), and the only opened minute split is a commercial 初中英语 template — 导入 5 / 新授 20 / 巩固练习 15 / 总结作业 5 of 45 ([亿图 教学设计模板](https://mm.edrawsoft.cn/template/193411)), which is secondary.

### The reform models add hard time rules and a try-first order

The textbooks' 讲授 skeleton was overwritten in practice by the 1980s reform models, which the same textbooks now teach. 邱学华's twelve tips for a good lesson include "及早出示课题，提出教学目标", "控制教师讲话，多留练习时间 — 教师讲话'不超过10分钟'，留出30分钟让学生活动", "内容不要太多，把握教学节奏", "'马马虎虎做十道题，不如认认真真做一道题'", "先练后讲，练在当堂；边练边讲，订正在当堂", and tiered practice ("优秀生难度高、数量多；学困生难度低、数量少") ([邱学华 上好一堂课的十二个诀窍](https://fuzhong.nwafu.edu.cn/tzgg/292524.htm)). His 尝试教学法 runs 准备练习 → 出示尝试题 → 自学课本 → 尝试练习 → 学生讨论 → 教师讲解 → 第二次尝试练习 under "先试后导、先练后讲、先学后教", with the teacher explaining "根据学生的尝试练习情况以及教材的重点进行有针对性讲解" ([教师教育学报 2023 专访](https://xbgjxt.swu.edu.cn/article/doi/10.13718/j.cnki.jsjy.2023.04.001?viewType=HTML)). 洋思中学 began by reserving **the last 15 minutes** of every period for pupils' own work, then replaced the teacher's **30-minute** lecture with 先学, then turned 后教 into 兵教兵 ([曹伟林, 中国教育报 2022](http://www.jyb.cn/rmtzgjyb/202202/t20220209_678534.html)); its 当堂训练 has two rounds — 模仿练习 (the pupil may look back at the example) and 达标检测 ("完全独立地、快节奏地完成") — and 后教 means pupils first resolve difficulties among themselves and the teacher "再作点拨", with "堂堂清、天天清、周周清" as the remediation system ([CERNET 2006](https://www.cernet.edu.cn/xxh/fei/xin_xi_zi_xun/importantnews/200603/t20060323_158424.shtml)). 卢仲衡's 自学辅导 gives 启 and 结 **about 15 minutes** together and leaves the middle **30 minutes** uninterrupted for 读、练、知 "快者快学，慢者慢学", insists on 变式复习 over mechanical repetition ("用机械的方法重复不如用变式复习效果好"), steps that are "高而可攀", and immediate knowledge of results ([卢仲衡 1998](https://ir.psych.ac.cn/bitstream/311026/9615/2/%E4%B8%89%E5%8D%81%E4%B8%89%E5%B9%B4%E8%87%AA%E5%AD%A6%E8%BE%85%E5%AF%BC%E6%95%99%E5%AD%A6%E7%A0%94%E7%A9%B6%E7%9A%84%E5%9B%9E%E9%A1%BE%E4%B8%8E%E5%B1%95%E6%9C%9B.pdf)); 杜郎口 cuts a lesson into **six links of 6-8 minutes** ([中国新闻网 2014](https://www.chinanews.com.cn/edu/2014/01-22/5765885.shtml)); 魏书生's 六步 is 定向、自学、讨论、答疑、自测、自结 under "教育的最高境界是让学生自育自学" ([中国教师报 2023](http://www.jyb.cn/rmtzgjsb/202307/t20230711_2111068301.html)). 顾泠沅's own criteria show the drift from 1982 ("提问与书面检查正确率高") to 2007 ("教学要求适合学生最近发展区…根据学生反应随时调节教学的节奏与步调; 避免不必要的重复讲述和大运动量训练"), and his county-wide 8th-grade tests moved the mean score rate from **45.27 % (1990, n = 3000) to 58.83 % (2007, n = 4349)**, with the gain at the computation and comprehension levels while "分析水平…尚无明显提高" ([顾泠沅 WALS 2007](https://eduhk.hk/wals/conference07/resources/wals07/2_29112007_gu.pdf)). The skeleton common to all four verified models — state the goal, the student tries, the teacher teaches only what the attempt exposed, practise and correct in the same lesson, summarise common errors — is what the agent should encode; the 导学案 line adds the design rule of three nodes (切入点 = the known or lived link, 研讨点 = the lesson's 重点, 迁移点 = 仿写 or 变式) and the gradient "低起点，小跨度、上台阶" ([中国教师报 2020](http://www.jyb.cn/rmtzgjsb/202006/t20200602_333838.html)). Errors are handled as the 学记 prescribes: 顾泠沅's worked example lets two pupils' factorisations of x⁶−1 yield different-looking answers and asks "同一题目，两种方法做怎么答案不一样呢？" so that verification exposes a new method ([顾泠沅 WALS 2007](https://eduhk.hk/wals/conference07/resources/wals07/2_29112007_gu.pdf)), and institutional rules require that "对作业中的错误要督促学生自行修正" ([上海师大 教师课堂教学行为规范](https://psy.shnu.edu.cn/46/54/c30437a804436/page.htm)).

On age and differentiation the 教育心理学 texts tie bands to thinking type — 6-7 to 11-12 "形象抽象思维", 11-12 to 14-15 "以经验型为主的抽象逻辑思维", 14-15 to 17-18 "以理论型为主的抽象逻辑思维" — and instruct that content and strategy "要遵循量力性原则…教育要适应'最近发展区'，走在发展的前面" ([清华大学出版社 教育心理学 第二章](http://www.tup.tsinghua.edu.cn/upload/books/yz/071572-01.pdf)); a prep-site summary (snippet only) places the 关键年龄 of the primary transition "大约在四年级(10～11岁)" ([233 小学生心理发展](https://www.233.com/teacher/xinlixue/20081003/144545956.html)). Policy fixes the ends of the range: 一年级上学期 is an 入学适应期 taught "游戏化、生活化、综合化" at 零起点, with "坚决纠正超标教学、盲目追赶进度" ([教育部 幼小衔接 2021](http://www.moe.gov.cn/srcsite/A06/s3327/202104/t20210408_525137.html)); grades 1-2 get **no written homework** ("可在校内安排适当巩固练习"), grades 3-6 **at most 60 minutes a day**, 初中 **at most 90 minutes**, homework should be 分层、弹性、个性化 with no 重复性、惩罚性 items, and teachers must 全批全改 ([教基厅函〔2021〕13号](http://www.moe.gov.cn/srcsite/A06/s3321/202104/t20210425_528077.html)). 卢仲衡's four learner types (快而准、快而不准、慢而准、慢而不准) each get a different handling — the fast-inaccurate need "老师多看着他们", the slow-accurate need permission to reread and benefit most, the slow-inaccurate get 重点辅导 that "仍然要求学生自读课文，自做练习" ([卢仲衡 1998](https://ir.psych.ac.cn/bitstream/311026/9615/2/%E4%B8%89%E5%8D%81%E4%B8%89%E5%B9%B4%E8%87%AA%E5%AD%A6%E8%BE%85%E5%AF%BC%E6%95%99%E5%AD%A6%E7%A0%94%E7%A9%B6%E7%9A%84%E5%9B%9E%E9%A1%BE%E4%B8%8E%E5%B1%95%E6%9C%9B.pdf)).

For the agent this means the lesson-generation schema should be the 教案 plus 说课: a one-line 学情 statement (what the student already knows and the likely misconception), one or two 重点 and exactly one 难点 each with its 依据, goals at a stated verb level, a planned 正板书 outline written before teaching, a tiered 作业 inside the legal caps, and after the lesson a 反思 note; the delivery schema is 定向 (≤1 minute) → 尝试 → 后教 on what failed → two-round 当堂训练 → 自结, with teacher speech budgeted at no more than a quarter of the lesson and no explanation block longer than 6-8 minutes without a student action. Several standard items remain unverified in the notes — 王道俊/郭文安 第七版, the 凯洛夫 五环节 wording, 魏书生's step contents, the 主/副板书 layout rule as mainstream teacher-press text, the national 青教赛 rubric — and the knowledge base should carry their verification status rather than present them as sourced.

## International texts supply the dials: 80 % success, 3-second waits, no person-praise

Rosenshine's ten principles are the international spine, and they arrive with numbers. The most effective mathematics teachers observed began lessons with a **5-8-minute** review, spent about **23 minutes of a 40-minute** period on presentation, questioning and worked examples versus **11 minutes** for the least effective (who asked only nine questions and then handed out worksheets), obtained **82 %** correct answers during practice versus **73 %** ("the optimal success rate…appears to be about 80 percent"), kept desk contacts during independent practice to **30 seconds or less**, and reviewed weekly and monthly ([Rosenshine 2012](https://www.aft.org/sites/default/files/Rosenshine.pdf)). The prescriptions attached are concrete: present "only small amounts of new material at a time" and master each point before the next; model with thinking aloud and worked examples that state "the underlying principles for these steps"; alternate worked examples with problems and use partially completed problems; check understanding by having the student summarise, repeat the procedure or explain a step — never by "Are there any questions?"; stop practice if errors cluster because "practice can be a disaster if students are practicing errors"; keep independent practice on "the same material as the guided practice"; and do experiential activities "after, not before, the basic material was learned" ([Rosenshine 2012](https://www.aft.org/sites/default/files/Rosenshine.pdf)). The Sutton Trust review rates the principles as a summary of "at least 40 years of research" whose observational base is backed "for each of these principles" by experiments in which training teachers in the behaviour improved outcomes ([Coe et al. 2014](https://www.suttontrust.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/10/What-Makes-Great-Teaching-REPORT.pdf)).

The lesson-design frameworks give near-identical skeletons and one design rule. Hunter's elements — anticipatory set, objective/purpose, input, modeling, checking for understanding, guided practice, independent practice, closure — are explicitly "not a recipe to be followed step by step" ([Wolfe 1987](https://files.ascd.org/staticfiles/ascd/pdf/journals/ed_lead/el_198702_wolfe.pdf); [CSUN Hunter](https://csun.edu/science/ref/plans/lesson_design_hunter.html)); Gagné's nine events add "stimulate recall of prior learning", learning guidance with "examples and non-examples, analogies", and feedback typed as confirmatory, evaluative, remedial ("directs toward the answer without revealing it") or analytic ([NIU on Gagné](https://www.niu.edu/citl/resources/guides/instructional-guide/gagnes-nine-events-of-instruction.shtml)). Understanding by Design contributes the rule that the evidence of understanding is designed before the activities ("think like assessors before designing specific units and lessons") and the warning that "understanding cannot simply be told; the learner has to actively construct meaning (or misconceptions and forgetfulness will ensue)" ([McTighe & Wiggins 2012](https://files.ascd.org/staticfiles/ascd/pdf/siteASCD/publications/UbD_WhitePaper0312.pdf)); the revised Bloom taxonomy supplies the verb ladder — remember, understand, apply, analyze, evaluate, create — for grading question difficulty, with Krathwohl's caveat that the hierarchy is not strict ([Krathwohl 2002](https://rvc-web-app01.live01.azure.rvc.ac.uk/Media/Default/About/Academic%20Quality,%20Regulations%20and%20Procedures/Academic%20Quality%20Assurance%20and%20Enhancement%20Procedures/Module%20Development%20and%20Approval/A%20Revision%20of%20Bloom's%20Taxonomy%20-%20An%20Overview.%20David%20R.%20Krathwohl.pdf)). For a 3-5-section lesson this maps cleanly: section 1 = set, purpose and recall; sections 2-3 = input and modelling, then guided practice with checks; the last section = independent exercise and closure, with the final exercise written first and every board point checked against it.

Black and Wiliam turn checking into a discipline. Their 1998 review claims formative-assessment innovations "typically produced 0.4-0.7 effect sizes" and help low achievers most, warns that "if pupils are given only marks or grades, they do not benefit from the feedback", and prescribes wait time, frequent short tests ("any new learning should first be tested within about a week of a first encounter"; an end-of-unit test "is pointless; it is too late to work with the results") ([Black & Wiliam 1998](https://kappanonline.org/inside-the-black-box-raising-standards-through-classroom-assessment/)); the 2009 theory paper defines formative practice as evidence "elicited, interpreted, and used…to make decisions about the next steps" and names one-to-one teaching's "real-time adjustments" as the synchronous case ([Black & Wiliam 2009](https://kclpure.kcl.ac.uk/ws/files/9119063/Black2009_Developing_the_theory_of_formative_assessment.pdf)). The hinge question is the operational unit: asked mid-lesson, answered by every student "within two minutes", read by the teacher "in 30 seconds or less", and designed so that "students with the right thinking and students with the wrong thinking" give different answers ([Wiliam 2015](https://www.ascd.org/el/articles/designing-great-hinge-questions)); closed diagnostic questions ("Would your mass be the same on the moon?") are valuable when "each question is focused on a specific misconception", prepared "upstream" ([Wiliam 2004](https://www.dylanwiliam.org/Dylan_Wiliams_website/Papers_files/IAEA%2004%20paper.pdf)). Feedback should be something to do — "five of their answers were wrong, and…they should find them and fix them", with "a dot in the margin for each error" for stronger students and the error's location for weaker ones — and teachers should change habits by "never asking a question without waiting three seconds" ([Wiliam, Cambridge AfL keynote](https://www.dylanwiliam.org/Dylan_Wiliams_website/Papers_files/Cambridge%20AfL%20keynote.doc)). Rowe's wait-time research, as summarised, found that once average wait time passed roughly **2.7 seconds** response length rose "by between 300% and 700%" and "I don't know" replies fell, with the pause after the student's answer described as "a longer, more important one" ([Structural Learning on Rowe 1986](https://www.structural-learning.com/research-briefings/wait-time-three-seconds)). The 0.4-0.7 range is a 1998 narrative figure; later re-analyses were not opened and the notes flag it as contested.

Hattie and Timperley's feedback model fixes the grading template. Feedback answers "Where am I going? How am I going? Where to next?" at four levels — task, process, self-regulation and self — of which "FS is the least effective, FR and FP are powerful in terms of deep processing", and about 90 % of teachers' questions sit at the task level ([Hattie & Timperley 2007](https://www.uky.edu/~gmswan3/575/Hattie_Timperly_2007.pdf)). Praise scores **0.12-0.14** in their tables, "no praise has a greater impact on achievement than praise", mixing "Good boy, that is correct" with task feedback "dilutes the power" of the correction, tangible rewards correlate **−0.34** with performance (−0.78 when controlling), and — the one age-specific finding in the whole international set — older students read praise after success "as an indication that the teacher perceived their ability to be low" while younger students read it as a sign of high ability ([Hattie & Timperley 2007](https://www.uky.edu/~gmswan3/575/Hattie_Timperly_2007.pdf)). Two further rules matter for an agent: "if students lack necessary knowledge, further instruction is more powerful than feedback information", so a missing prerequisite triggers a re-teach rather than a longer comment; and immediate correction speeds acquisition of a new step while delayed feedback helps on difficult items (Clariana: **−0.06** easy, **0.35** midrange, **1.17** difficult) ([Hattie & Timperley 2007](https://www.uky.edu/~gmswan3/575/Hattie_Timperly_2007.pdf)).

The cognitive-science texts explain why the loop works and add rules for a talking video with a board. Cognitive load theory: "students provided problems to solve learn less than students presented identical problems with worked examples", and as knowledge grows "having a student complete problem-solving tasks will become more effective" (the expertise reversal that makes prior knowledge, not age, the switch between worked example and problem); do not read aloud text that is on screen (redundancy), chunk speech because it is transient, integrate labels into diagrams, and narrate diagrams rather than caption them ([Sweller interview, Teacher magazine](https://www.teachermagazine.com/au_en/articles/cognitive-load-theory-teaching-strategies)). Willingham: "memory is the residue of thought", so the student must think about meaning; "we understand new things in the context of things we already know, and most of what we know is concrete"; structure a lesson "in the format of a story, complete with…causality, conflict, complications"; "provide examples and ask students to compare them"; and "children are more alike than different in terms of how they think and learn" ([Young-Jones review of Willingham](https://www.erpjournal.net/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/ERPV40_Young-Jones_2013_Willingham_Book_Review.pdf)). Make It Stick: retrieval beats rereading, spaced and interleaved practice beat massed practice "although interleaving feels slower", generation (attempt before being shown) helps, and instructors should "explain why pedagogical choices create difficulty" ([APA blog on Make It Stick](https://blog.apaonline.org/2020/02/19/takeaways-from-make-it-stick-the-science-of-successful-learning/)). Mayer's multimedia principles are the board-and-video rules: graphics plus narration beats graphics plus narration plus duplicate on-screen text; put words next to the picture they describe; signal what matters; segment at the learner's pace; pre-teach the names of key components; use a conversational style and a natural human voice; and "instructor images don't necessarily improve outcomes", though on-screen instructors with human-like gesture help ([educationaltechnology.net on Mayer](https://educationaltechnology.net/?p=1878); [OSU multimedia principles](https://u.osu.edu/multimedialearning/?p=11)). The board is therefore labels, steps and figures, never a transcript; the talking head is justified by rapport, not learning gains, and the synthetic voice is a known cost.

Lemov's techniques are the most granular moves with verifiable definitions. No Opt Out: when a student cannot or will not answer, "make the sequence end as often as possible with the student giving a right or valid answer" through four formats (teacher gives the answer and the student repeats; a peer gives it; the teacher gives a cue such as "the first thing he should do"; a peer gives a cue), with "I don't know" acceptable only as "I don't know; however…" plus a best try ([TLAC Field Guide, Technique 1](https://teachlikeachampion.org/wp-content/uploads/Field-Guide-sample-chapter.pdf)); Stretch It follows a right answer with "another question to let him apply the idea" ([TLAC blog: Stretch It](https://teachlikeachampion.org/blog/tlac-2-0-adding-stretch-it-to-no-opt-out/)); Show Call projects a student's written work and asks "What did they do here?" ([TLAC blog: Show Call](https://teachlikeachampion.org/blog/katie-mcnickles-show-call/)); Everybody Writes makes "every student…answer a question instead of just a few who volunteer" ([TLAC tag: Everybody Writes](https://teachlikeachampion.org/tag/everybody-writes/)); Precise Praise names "specific actions a student is taking", separates acknowledgement from praise and prefers private individual praise ([TLAC tag: Precise Praise](https://teachlikeachampion.org/tag/precise-praise/)); Retrieval Practice rests on "understanding is not learning until it is encoded in long-term memory" ([TLAC tag: Retrieval Practice](https://teachlikeachampion.org/tag/retrieval-practice/)). The ten techniques new or revised in the 2021 third edition include Exemplar Planning (write the exemplar answer before the lesson), Delivery Moves (decide in advance whether a question is answered by talk, cold call or writing, and say so), Silent Solo (brief individual writing launched with one word), Private Individual Correction, Phase Reading (teacher-led read-aloud in every subject) and Knowledge Organisers quizzed "several times a week" ([TES interview with Lemov](https://www.tes.com/magazine/teaching-learning/general/10-new-teaching-techniques-doug-lemov-wants-you-know)). Right Is Right, Format Matters and Warm/Strict could only be partly verified online and must be checked against the book.

Evidence quality is uneven and the vetoes matter as much as the prescriptions. The Sutton Trust lists practices "whose use is not supported by research evidence": "use praise lavishly" (praise for an easy task "conveys a message of the teacher's low expectations"), "allow learners to discover key ideas for themselves" ("if teachers want them to learn new ideas, knowledge or methods they need to teach them directly"), ability grouping, re-reading and highlighting, "address issues of confidence and low aspirations before you try to teach content" ("start getting them to succeed and their motivation and confidence should increase"), learning styles ("no benefits") and "ensure learners are always active" ([Coe et al. 2014](https://www.suttontrust.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/10/What-Makes-Great-Teaching-REPORT.pdf)). Hattie's rankings (feedback **0.70**, teacher clarity **0.75**, classroom discussion **0.82**, direct instruction **0.60**, worked examples **0.37**, interleaving **0.21** in the 2017 list) agree with Rosenshine and Wiliam at the top ([visible-learning.org 2017 list](https://visible-learning.org/backup-hattie-ranking-256-effects-2017/)), but statisticians show the method averaged incommensurable effect sizes, produced impossible negative probabilities and uses an arbitrary benchmark — "no one should replicate this methodology" ([Bergeron & Rivard 2017](https://mje.mcgill.ca/article/download/9475/7228/30586)) — so where Hattie's low numbers for worked examples and interleaving contradict controlled cognitive-science findings, the experimental literature wins. The expert-novice studies point at what experts do differently: experienced ESL teachers asked **32.2** questions per period versus **24.3** for novices, experienced mathematics teachers used **8.2** scaffolds per period versus **4.3** and "divided the original questions into more simple partial questions", and gave examples **2.5** times per period "whereas novices did not use this at all" — small samples, as the reviewer warns, but the direction is consistent ([Ropo 2004 on Berliner](https://www.mv.helsinki.fi/home/hotulain/Expertise.ROPOkopio.pdf)).

The apparent conflict between the Chinese 先学后教 order and the international veto on discovery-first dissolves once 先学 is read as a diagnostic rather than a discovery method: the 尝试题 is short, the student is expected to reach only what prior knowledge allows, and the teacher-led explanation and comparison that follow are mandatory. The agent therefore chooses per section: a full worked example first when the student lacks the prerequisite (novice, new procedure, grades 1-2), and a bounded 2-3-minute attempt first when the diagnostic shows the prerequisites are present. The two controls to log from the first pilot are the exercise success rate (target about 80 %, *cited*; below roughly 70 % add worked examples, above roughly 90 % fade support — both thresholds *proposed targets*) and the silence held after each question (at least 3 seconds, *cited*).

## China's masters converge on nine moves from 化错 to 先学后教

Four honour systems identify China's outstanding teachers, and the format of the competitions is itself a design spec. 特级教师 is a provincial title (first cohort 1978); 全国教书育人楷模 has been awarded yearly since 2010, jointly by 中宣部 and 教育部 since 2020, with only a handful of K-12 subject teachers among each year's 10-12 ([共产党员网 2022 名单](https://www.12371.cn/2022/09/06/ARTI1662443893558815.shtml)); the 基础教育国家级教学成果奖 (2014, 2018, 2022) is the only honour that rewards a documented method — its 2022 round gave 570 basic-education awards, 71.2 % to front-line schools, with a "方向性问题" as a one-vote veto and an explicit statement that evaluators "不鼓励盲目提出新概念、新名词" ([教师〔2023〕4号](https://hudong.moe.gov.cn/srcsite/A10/s7000/202307/t20230724_1070571.html); [吕玉刚 interview, 中国教师报](http://www.jyb.cn/rmtzgjsb/202401/t20240103_2111140185.html)). The 全国中小学青年教师教学竞赛 (全国总工会 + 教育部) is the closest thing to a national standard of live craft: in the 2023 final each contestant submitted a syllabus, **ten lesson plans** and matching slides, then "随机抽取一个教学节段进行教学展示", scored separately on 教学设计 and 教学展示 ([教育部 2023-08-23](http://www.moe.gov.cn/jyb_xwfb/gzdt_gzdt/s5987/202308/t20230823_1075757.html)); the 2025 final required plans covering "不少于3种课型和2个年级" and drew on **1.08 million** young teachers from 80,000 schools ([教育部 2025-08-29](http://www.moe.gov.cn/jyb_xwfb/gzdt_gzdt/s5987/202508/t20250829_1411004.html)). An agent that can produce a coherent ten-lesson plan across lesson types and grades and then run any one 3-5-section segment on demand mirrors exactly what the competition rewards.

Primary 语文 runs as a lineage, and each link contributes a move. 斯霞 (南京师大附小, 68 years, named 最美奋斗者 in 2019) taught grades 1-2 literacy by 随课文分散识字 under "字不离词，词不离句" — the character learned inside the word inside the sentence of the text — and at 84 told a young teacher through the classroom window "黑板上的字小了点，后排同学看不清" ([澎湃 2019](https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_4959458)). 李吉林 (南通师范二附小; 2014 国家级教学成果特等奖 ([教师〔2014〕8号](http://www.moe.gov.cn/jyb_xxgk/xxgk/neirong/fenlei/sxml_jsgz/jsgz_sdjs/sdjs_yxjs/201409/t20140905_174749.html))) states her classroom essentials in her own words — 美 as the entry, 思 as the core, 情 as the link, 儿童活动 as the route, 周围世界 as the source — with six ways to create a situation: 图画再现、音乐渲染、表演体会、生活展现、实物演示、语言描绘 ([李吉林, 教育研究 2002](https://www.aisixiang.com/data/100090.html)). 于永正 (徐州; 江苏省特级 1985; the 教育部 held a national seminar on his 五重教学法 — 重情趣、重感悟、重积累、重迁移、重习惯) is documented doing 绘声绘色的范读, acting《狐狸与乌鸦》with pupils, drawing 简笔画 on the board, "蹲下来看学生", applauding a child who kicked a shuttlecock badly, and writing a 下水文 before assigning a composition; his pupil records that he taught reading aloud "一个字、一个词、一个句子甚至一个标点符号都不放过" and that "教育不是圈养，而是放牧" ([澎湃号 于永正](https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_28485552); [李虹霞, 中国教师报 2024](http://www.jyb.cn/rmtzgjsb/202404/t20240416_2111181994.html)). 贾志敏 (特级 1994; CCTV《贾老师教作文》1992) ran lessons "以学生为主，以训练为主，以激励为主" and taught composition by 高、趣、真、活、实 ([澎湃 2019](https://m.thepaper.cn/wifiKey_detail.jsp?contid=2962311&from=wifiKey)). 窦桂梅 (清华附小; 2014 一等奖 for 主题教学) "把教材打碎了重新组合成主题教学" (a grade-5 立志 theme linking《詹天佑》and《为中华崛起而读书》), wants the teacher to "逐步'退居后台'", and uses "看得见的工具来撬动，如思维导图、卡片" ([澎湃 2016 interview](https://m.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_1456211)). 薛法根's 组块教学 targets three learnables — 学会积累 (字族识记、词串诵读、句群比照), 学会阅读 (从段起步、因文而异、由篇达类), 学会表达 (现结构 → 用结构 → 化结构) — with examples from grades 3-6 ([薛法根, 福建教育 2015](https://www.aisixiang.com/data/91299.html)); 孙双金 gives the observable target state of a lesson, the 四小: 小脸通红、小眼发光、小手直举、小嘴常开 ([扬子晚报 2025](https://www.yzwb.net/news/yysp/202509/t20250930_271296.html)); 王崧舟 (诗意语文, 2100+ demonstration lessons in 29 provinces) builds lessons on one cultural image read at three levels ([浙江在线 2017](https://zjnews.zjol.com.cn/zjnews/hznews/201707/t20170731_4682424.shtml); [王崧舟 2025](https://m.163.com/dy/article/K2DJ2PO00516DKF5.html)). The common core is to put a concrete scene in front of the child, read aloud with feeling and have the child echo, keep one lesson to one learnable block, and make the child perform.

Primary mathematics has the richest documented micro-technique, and it centres on the error. 华应龙 (北京第二实验小学; 特级; 首批正高级; researching 化错 since 1992) defines it himself: turn classroom mistakes into resources so that "'事故'转变为'故事'", in three stages — 容 ("错是错，温暖地容"), 融 ("错不是错，智慧地融"), 荣 ("错还是错，自豪地荣") — with pupils keeping a 拾错集 and the maxim "可怕的不是学生犯错误，而是教师错误地对待学生的错误" ([华应龙, 光明日报 2015](https://epaper.gmw.cn/gmrb/html/2015-01/13/nw.D110000gmrb_20150113_1-15.htm)). Observed, this is a lesson that opens with a waiter's claim ("8加4等于12。孩子们，服务员的想法对吗？大家先独立思考，然后想办法验证"), greets a pupil's 1000÷(20+5) with "现在我们来欣赏……" and a chorus of "错得好！" before asking whether division distributes, and consoles a crying child with "尽量少犯差错，这是做人的准则；从来不犯差错，那是天使的梦想" ([人民日报 2019](http://society.people.com.cn/n1/2019/0721/c1008-31246394.html)). His pacing rules are the most directly imitable: each child's name on a card used all lesson; "刻意等待，等绝大部分学生举手了才指名回答"; telling eager hand-raisers "能憋住不说的孩子，将来更有成就…因为他心中有他人"; constant 为什么; "老师在课堂上要学会装傻…老师太聪明的话学生就会变'傻'"; planting small "事故"; and closing a lesson with "你们下次去，怎么点餐？下课啦！" ([中国教育报 2025](http://paper.jyb.cn/zgjyb/h5/html5/2025-04/08/content_144742_18418009.htm)). His teacher 张兴华 (江苏省特级 1984) supplies the cognitive-conflict template: after pupils grasp 平方厘米 he has them measure stamps and then "不露声色地" a desk, the unit fails, "认知平衡被打破", and the pupils themselves ask for a bigger unit — "始终制造认知冲突，并充分利用这种冲突把它转化为持续高涨的内驱力", "好的数学教学，要舍得让儿童在思考中'浪费'时间", "把新知之舟停泊在旧知'锚桩'" ([张兴华口述, 中国教师报 2020](http://www.jyb.cn/rmtzgjsb/202012/t20201215_382756.html)). 俞正强 (金华师范附小; 2024 全国教书育人楷模) teaches 种子课 under the slogan "把课上对" — a 《生活中的比》opened from football scores, exam marks, salt-water ratio and exchange rates — and argues that homework balloons when "老师没有把课上对" ([澎湃 2024](https://m.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_28814093); [新华网 2024 名单](https://www.news.cn/edu/20240906/ff95f1b23d804466983309609ecf0b47/c.html)). 黄爱华's 大问题 lesson on 倒数 opens with "大家对于倒数你有什么问题？" and sorts the pupils' questions into "什么是倒数？" and "怎么求倒数？" ([双流县实验小学 校本培训简讯](https://slypzx.com/uploadFolder/mystudio/2016/12/19/校本培训简讯_57002.doc), snippet only). For grades 1-2, 刘善娜 (press: 全国数学特级教师) rules "千万不要急着带孩子刷题，要放入大量的操作实践活动", teaches 画数学 ("看一条信息画一步图…读题完毕时作图也完毕") and 做数学 (cut a cube into eight with three cuts; fold an A4 into a die and guess the opposite face) ([澎湃号 小花生 2024](https://m.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_26476895)). 李烈 (北京第二实验小学; 1993 全国首届小学数学课堂教学大赛 一等奖第一名) insists love must be "得法、有效" — "爱的能力" — and frames 双主体育人 ([人民网 京华周刊 2011](http://paper.people.com.cn/jhzk/html/2011-05/01/content_959277.htm)). Primary English is thinly documented; the best verified account is 王咏梅's own four turns (江苏省特级): a toy-shop 情境 with props and costumes that won a 2001 provincial 优质课, her own verdict on the recording ("形式大于内容…华而不实") and turn to text content, a culture layer, and in 2020 an 阅读圈 with six pupil roles while she "退到旁边静静地观察" ([王咏梅, 中国教育报 2022](http://www.jyb.cn/rmtzgjyb/202206/t20220607_696175.html)). Across the maths masters one lesson shape recurs: a concrete situation with a hidden conflict, silent individual trial, a visible wrong answer treated as the lesson's best material, a peer-praise round ("谁来夸夸他"), then the rule.

Junior-high craft is dominated by sequence models that put the pupil's attempt before the teacher's explanation and a timed check inside the period. 魏书生's 六步 (定向 → 自学 → 讨论 → 答疑 → 自测 → 自结) remains textbook content, alongside his 语文知识树, the daily 日记 as "道德长跑", and a class-management system in which "人人有事干，事事有人干" ([中国教育报 2022](http://www.jyb.cn/rmtzgjyb/202203/t20220323_685920.html); [光明网 教育家 2019](https://jyj.gmw.cn/2019-07/04/content_32973748.htm)). 钱梦龙 (上海首批特级; 三主 = 学生为主体、教师为主导、训练为主线) insisted "语文教学是教学生读书的…必须给学生充分的读书时间", called 串讲 a theft of that time, wanted the teacher's pointers "只要三言两语，不要啰里啰唆", and built a 1981《故乡》lesson from **600+ questions** pupils wrote on slips after pre-reading ([中国教育报 2024](http://www.jyb.cn/rmtzgjyb/202406/t20240605_2111205162.html); [中国教师报 2024](http://www.jyb.cn/rmtzgjsb/202405/t20240528_2111201569.html)). 李庾南 (南通启秀中学 数学; on the same podium since 1957; 一等奖 in both 2014 and 2022 — "全国两获…一等奖的第一人") runs 自学·议论·引导 through 独立自学 → 群体议论 → 相机引导, distinguishes 议论 from 讨论 ("讨论往往是一个问题找到了答案就结束了，议论…可能又产生新的问题"), and defines the teacher's role as "不是告诉学生'这是什么'，而是点拨一下…使学生能明白'这是为什么'" ([中国教师报 2023](http://www.jyb.cn/rmtzgjsb/202303/t20230314_2111014383.html)); her own habits are "我研究和推敲每一句教学语言，努力做到准确、清晰而又简洁", 面批 of homework, preparing "这一节课我该怎么学，希望老师怎么讲", and "允许学生犯错，帮助学生纠错" ([李庾南, 人民教育 2024](http://www.jyb.cn/rmtzcg/xwy/wzxw/202409/t20240913_2111245227.html)). 孙维刚 (北京二十二中; taught the same pupils 初一 to 高三 across three cycles) built 结构教学法 on 一题多解、多解归一、多题归一, had pupils guess and prove theorems rather than receive them, set no homework, and warned "题海战术，是覆舟之术…题不在多但求精彩" ([中国教育报 via 人民网 2015](http://edu.people.com.cn/n/2015/0813/c1053-27455474.html)). The whole-school models add the time discipline already described — 洋思's 揭示目标 → 自学 → 兵教兵 → last 15 minutes of 当堂作业, with the critique that a pre-set 教学案 can become "新时代另一种'填鸭式'" ([李仁甫 2014](http://edu.people.com.cn/n/2014/0219/c1053-24408466.html)); 杜郎口's "10+35" and six links ([中国新闻网 2014](https://www.chinanews.com.cn/edu/2014/01-22/5765885.shtml)); and 青浦's 尝试指导—效果回授 with 变式 ([顾泠沅, 华东师大学报 2022](https://xbjk.ecnu.edu.cn/CN/Y2022/V40/I4/103)). A school-authored account of 竹山中学's 适性课堂 gives per-subject routines the agent can reuse — 数学 "通过真实情境或问题导入新课…分层学习"; 英语 "情境导入…角色扮演"; 物理化学 "生动的实验或问题导入…小结检测环节"; 道德与法治 in five steps 主题概念辨析、核心知识解析、主题话题探讨、经典试题例析、主题寄语撰写 ([中国教育报 2025](http://paper.jyb.cn/zgjyb/h5/html5/2025-03/16/content_144743_18349092.htm)). Tone rules come from 李镇西 and 程红兵, who attack staged 公开课 — "在掌握规范之后，再表演规范，是在演戏" and "观摩课应当在常态下进行…失去常态，就失去了真实，失去了可学性" ([程红兵、李镇西 2016](https://www.aisixiang.com/data/100208.html)).

Senior-high masters talk less about steps and more about what the lesson is for, and their imitable moves are preparation habits. 于漪 (first-cohort 特级 1978; 2010 楷模; 2019 人民教育家, the only basic-education recipient; about 2000 public lessons, none repeated) wrote out every sentence of a lesson, revised, memorised and then colloquialised it, rehearsed it "过电影" on the way to school, refused to copy the 教参 ("不吃别人嚼过的馍"), used 周杰伦's《青花瓷》and the 2010 世博会 as material because "时代的活水要在课堂上流淌", and was driven to research by a pupil's remark "于老师，你的课我很喜欢听，但是我自己没有学会" ([教育部 2021 先进事迹](http://www.moe.gov.cn/jyb_xwfb/moe_2082/2021/2021_zl37/shideshiji/202105/t20210511_530839.html); [教育部 2010 楷模](http://www.moe.gov.cn/jyb_xwfb/xw_zt/moe_357/s3582/s4672/s4728/s4729/201010/t20101028_110449.html)). 程翔 describes abandoning "把课文掰开了揉碎了，讲深讲透，面面俱到" for 叶圣陶's "教材是一个例子…举一反三", distinguishing 教读课 from 自读课 ([程翔, 中华读书报 2020](http://www.jyb.cn/rmtzcg/xwy/wzxw/202011/t20201116_373898.html)). 章建跃 (人教A版 chief editor) sets the mathematics rule — "推理是数学的'命根子'，运算是数学的'童子功'", teaching as "系统的（逻辑）思维训练", with "如何发现数学对象、如何提出数学问题作为教学的关键任务" ([北师大数学学院 2017 讲座](https://math.bnu.edu.cn/xzbg/jcxylt/218849.htm)). 任勇 opens a session with a rope-through-cup puzzle two volunteers fail and then names the move ("这其实是一根绳子和一个洞的关系，大家要有逆向思维") under 玩中育智 ([中国教育新闻网 2025](http://www.jyb.cn/rmtzcg/xwy/wzxw/202504/t20250430_2111339397.html)). 李惠军 (上海 历史特级 1994) diagnoses the failure mode of contest lessons — a quarter "克隆再造所谓'思想方法'" until "核心内容缺失、关键人物缺位、故事情节缺略" — and holds up a 明治维新 lesson built on 西乡隆盛's life with layered questions, praised for "人物、情节和心态刻画" and faulted for "少了实证力量，忽视了第一手材料的印证" ([李惠军, 历史教学 2015](https://m.sohu.com/a/128183340_629818)). In science, 顾雨薇's 光的直线传播 opens with 画兔点睛 — one prop, two phenomena, "制造认知冲突" ([解放网 via 人民网 2024](http://sh.people.com.cn/n2/2024/0124/c138654-40724477.html)); chemistry guidance insists "要让学生自己动手做实验，不能教师动手学生看" ([楚得群, 中国教育报 2023](http://www.jyb.cn/rmtzgjyb/202306/t20230630_2111062695.html)); 道德与法治 uses 单元立意 in four moves (回顾知识—集思广益—问题解决—能力提升) and 行为体验 (write about your own parent after《背影》) ([袁小梅, 中国民族教育 2025](http://www.jyb.cn/rmtzcg/xwy/wzxw/202505/t20250509_2111341552.html)). Top schools' "models" are mostly curriculum architecture rather than classroom scripts: 十一学校's "4000多名學生擁有4000多張不同的課表" and 2014 特等奖 ([風傳媒 2014](https://www.storm.mg/article/29006); [教师〔2014〕8号](http://www.moe.gov.cn/jyb_xxgk/xxgk/neirong/fenlei/sxml_jsgz/jsgz_sdjs/sdjs_yxjs/201409/t20140905_174749.html)); 人大附中's 一核·两翼·三层级 and 30 years of 研究性学习 with a《研究性学习过程记录手册》([人民教育 2024](http://www.jyb.cn/rmtzcg/xwy/wzxw/202403/t20240327_2111173300.html); [中国教师报 2024](http://www.jyb.cn/rmtzgjsb/202406/t20240604_2111205135.html)); 衡水's classroom principles "无情境不教学""无应用不教学" ([衡水中学 网易号 2024](https://m.163.com/dy/article/J5H2NKNA04158FGB.html)). Nine moves recur across these masters regardless of subject, each documented in at least two independent sources: a 情境导入 with a hidden conflict (李吉林, 张兴华, 华应龙, 顾雨薇, 衡水); pupil-first attempt (洋思, 邱学华, 李庾南, 钱梦龙, 魏书生, 青浦, 孙维刚); one big question or block per section (黄爱华, 薛法根, 窦桂梅, 章建跃); error as material (华应龙, 李庾南, 洋思 兵教兵); 变式 and 一题多解 (青浦, 孙维刚, 任勇, 李庾南); a structured board or visible tool (华应龙, 窦桂梅, 斯霞); reading aloud and performing (于永正, 王咏梅, 孙双金, 钱梦龙); specific praise of the move rather than the person (于永正, 华应龙, 俞正强); and an in-lesson check before closing (洋思, 魏书生 自测, 邱学华 第二次尝试, 杜郎口 达标测评). The anti-patterns the masters themselves name are equally usable as a veto list: staged perfection (程红兵, 李镇西), 串讲 that steals reading time (钱梦龙), 题海 (孙维刚), 刷题 in grades 1-2 (刘善娜), 泛方法论 in history (李惠军), form over content (王咏梅 on her own prize lesson), the pre-set 教学案 as new 填鸭 (李仁甫), and "老师太聪明学生就变傻" (华应龙). None of these masters' effect claims (洋思's 100 % rates, 孙维刚's 55 % into 北大/清华) comes from a controlled study; they are school or press claims and should be treated as such.

## Abroad, awards document access while Shanghai, Japan and DI document mechanics

International teaching prizes are a weak source of lesson mechanics and a good source of climate rules. Across the Global Teacher Prize winners 2015-2026 the documented classroom moves are approach-level: Nancie Atwell's middle-school students "read an average of 40 books a year, choose which books they read, and write prolifically" in a reading-writing workshop of mini-lessons and conferences ([Wikipedia: Atwell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nancie_Atwell)); Hanan Al Hroub's play-based method for refugee-camp primary children "aimed at resolving violence and tension" ([Wikipedia: Al Hroub](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanan_Al_Hroub)); Andria Zafirakou learned basic phrases in about 35 of her pupils' home languages, used art "as a language-free method of communication" and states the rule "Build the relationship, build that trust. And then everything else can happen" ([Wikipedia: Zafirakou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andria_Zafirakou)); Ranjitsinh Disale added QR codes to primary textbooks linking to "audio poems, video lectures, assignments and stories" ([Wikipedia: Disale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ranjitsinh_Disale)); Mansour Al Mansour "connects education to real-world issues…through project-based learning" and "tailors his methods to meet individual student needs" ([Global Teacher Prize page](https://globalteacherprize.org/pages/mansour-al-mansour)). US National Teachers of the Year are cited mostly for relevance, identity and civic purpose — Rebecka Peterson's daily "One Good Thing" habit and "connections and providing individual support" ([OKC Fox 2023](https://okcfox.com/news/local/union-high-school-math-teacher-named-2023-national-teacher-of-the-year)); Leon Smith's "a teacher's superpower is the ability to see things in students that they do not see in themselves" ([CCSSO 2026](https://ccsso.org/press-releases/pennsylvania-history-teacher-named-2026-national-teacher-year)); Tabatha Rosproy's inclusive preschool where social-emotional learning comes first ([CEC blog 2020](https://exceptionalchildren.org/blog/inclusive-preschool-teacher-tabatha-rosproy-named-2020-national-teacher-year)). The only award texts that name lesson-level moves are Singapore's 2007 President's Award citations: "in-class dialogue sessions" and a problem-posing strategy that produced "marked improvement" in P6 mathematics, a maths trail at the zoo, an "X Challenge" times-table game, and drama and story-telling in secondary English ([Singapore MOE 2007 release](https://www.nas.gov.sg/archivesonline/data/pdfdoc/20070830998.pdf)). The pattern is consistent with the age split: winners who teach 6-11 are cited for safety, play and attendance; winners who teach 16-18 for curriculum relevance and college access.

The mechanics come from four documented systems. What English observers recorded in Shanghai-taught lessons is very close to the agent's format: "each lesson is designed in minute detail; each step choreographed; each question planned meticulously, and follow-up questions, according to a pupil's first response, also planned"; **35-minute** lessons on one idea; "high expectations of speaking in full sentences and mathematical language e.g - addend + addend = sum"; "construction of 'rules' (generalities) with whole class repetition"; the same stem sentence across contexts ("China is the whole and Shanghai is a part", then a journey, an area, six swans) with an error corrected because "the whole and the parts need to be the same noun"; a Y2 subtraction lesson that goes picture story → words → 4 − 1 = 3 → "what the 4 represents" with "the concrete and the abstract to be represented together"; a mid-lesson deeper task (动脑筋) for fast finishers — "this is not acceleration - but deepening"; and "same day intervention" so that "learners begin the next day at the same starting point" ([NCETM Shanghai report 2014](https://nrich.maths.org/content/id/10757/Shanghai_teachers_special_report_Nov_2014.pdf)). At secondary level the same exchange documented "structured teacher input (5 minutes or so at a time), interspersed with purposeful pupil activity on tasks employing procedural variation", "no differentiation of activities or content", precise terms ("product", "quotient", "reciprocal"), deep questioning "requiring the pupils to articulate how they worked out", and results "generalised algebraically" ([NCETM secondary feature](https://www.ncetm.org.uk/features/teaching-mathematics-for-mastery-at-secondary-school)); NCETM codified this as the Five Big Ideas — coherence, representation and structure, mathematical thinking, fluency, and variation that "draw[s] closer attention to a key feature…through varying some elements while keeping others constant" ([NCETM Five Big Ideas](https://www.ncetm.org.uk/teaching-for-mastery/mastery-explained/five-big-ideas-in-teaching-for-mastery/)). The evidential caveat is important: the DfE's longitudinal evaluation found that "analyses of impact of participation in the MTE alone do not indicate any effect on attainment, either at KS1 or at KS2", with only exploratory KS1 gains in the most involved schools ([Boylan et al. 2019](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/5c49b38340f0b61717193d2d/MTE_main_report.pdf)) — so these are documented practices of a high-performing system, not proven transfers.

Japanese structured problem solving is the mirror image of Direct Instruction and the strongest documented form of "try first". A Japanese lesson "is designed around solving a single problem to achieve a single objective", the teacher "presents a problem to the students without first demonstrating how to solve the problem", and the three defining features are carefully selected problems, extensive discussion (neriage) and blackboard practice (bansho) whose purpose is "to compare, contrast, and discuss ideas that students present" ([Takahashi 2006](https://www.criced.tsukuba.ac.jp/math/apec/apec2006/pdf/takahashi_ppt_1.pdf)). During work time teachers "use a seating chart of the class to jot down how each student approaches the problem as well as to plan how to lead the discussion", begin the discussion with the most common method, "usually do not say that the answers are right or wrong" at that stage, ask for common ideas and differences and then for each method's advantages and limitations ([Takahashi 2008](https://lessonresearch.net/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/Characteristics-of-Japanese-Mathematics-Lessons-2.pdf)). An observed 32-minute lesson ran hatsumon 0:00-6:27, individual work to 7:45, neriage to 21:20, group work to 30:00 and matome to 32:00, with the board written "progressively without erasing" so that the final board is "a comprehensive record of the lesson's structure" ([Batteau, Miyakawa & Ryu 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12145323/)). The TIMSS 1995 video study quantified the contrast at grade 8: Japanese students spent **44 %** of their time inventing new solutions and **41 %** practising routine procedures versus **96 %** practising in Germany and **90 %** in the US, **53 %** of Japanese lessons contained proofs versus **0 %** of US lessons, and US lessons were "less coherent" and "more frequently interrupted" ([NCES TIMSS Video Study](https://nces.ed.gov/pubs99/timssvid/index.asp)); Hiebert's gloss is that in Japan "confusion is an expected part of what students are going to confront" ([APM Reports 2015](https://apmreports.org/episode/2015/08/27/an-american-way-of-teaching)). Direct Instruction sits at the other pole and is the most fully specified technique base: field-tested scripts, examples and non-examples juxtaposed to achieve "faultless communication", unison responses on a signal at "a rate of 10 to 14 responses per minute", errors "corrected immediately" ([Athabasca: DI method](https://psyc.athabascau.ca/open/engelmann/direct.php); [Stein & Rolf, Chartered College](https://my.chartered.college/impact_article/the-case-for-direct-instruction/)), "only 10% of the material is new, while the remaining 90% is a review" ([Wikipedia: Direct instruction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Direct_instruction)), placement by skill and the principle that "low performers and disadvantaged learners must be taught at a faster rate than typically occurs if they are to catch up" ([NIFDI](https://www.nifdi.org/what-is-di/basic-philosophy)); in Project Follow Through DI "was first in reading, math, spelling and language" and its students "also demonstrated higher self-esteem", and Stockard's 2018 synthesis of 318 studies puts the average effect at "approximately 0.6 standard deviations" ([Athabasca: DI evidence](https://psyc.athabascau.ca/open/engelmann/direct-evid.php); [Wikipedia: Direct instruction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Direct_instruction)). Finland should not be presented as a source of distinctive technique: a survey of 400+ teachers found "the textbook is the crucial resource for exercises" and "almost 45 % of the teachers use the textbook simply as an exercise book" ([Lepik et al. 2015](https://ncm.gu.se/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/20_34_129156_lepik.pdf)).

The expert-teacher literature explains how to allocate the agent's attention. Hattie's synthesis describes experts as teachers who "can identify essential representations of their subject, can guide learning through classroom interactions, can monitor learning and provide feedback", who "optimise their automaticity to allow them to…respond to more complex, i.e., less routine, challenges" and who "transcend surface learning" ([Schrittesser 2014, quoting Hattie 2003](https://ger.mercy.edu/index.php/ger/article/view/108/79)); Berliner's programme stresses "adaptive or fluid expertise, automaticity and flexibility" and fast pattern recognition ([Berliner 2001 abstract](https://asu.elsevierpure.com/en/publications/learning-about-and-learning-from-expert-teachers)). For an AI teacher the translation is to script the routine layer — greeting, opening review, board template, exercise hand-off, closing — so that the live model's attention goes to the student's answer and its errors, which is where Coe's pedagogical-content-knowledge component ("understand the ways students think about the content…identify students' common misconceptions") says experts differ ([Coe et al. 2014](https://www.suttontrust.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/10/What-Makes-Great-Teaching-REPORT.pdf)). The DI and Japanese poles then become a per-section choice rather than a philosophy: DI-style faultless example sequences and fast call-and-response for new procedures, facts and the youngest band; a bounded problem-first attempt followed by an agent-supplied comparison of anticipated methods ("same idea or different idea? which breaks first when the numbers grow?") for concepts the student can reach from prior knowledge. Neriage for a class of one means the agent must bring the alternative methods it anticipated, and bansho maps onto the text board as a single per-lesson panel — problem top-left, the student's attempt verbatim in the middle, the rule on the right, never cleared mid-lesson — which is the same convention as the Chinese 正板书.

## Grade band changes tone, pace and abstraction, not the loop

The honest finding across all five notes is that the core texts prescribe the same cycle at every age and say little that is age-specific; what changes is documented mostly in policy, in one feedback-perception study, and in the masters' practice. Willingham's "children are more alike than different in terms of how they think and learn" and the expertise-reversal finding that prior knowledge, not age, sets the worked-example/problem balance ([Young-Jones on Willingham](https://www.erpjournal.net/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/ERPV40_Young-Jones_2013_Willingham_Book_Review.pdf); [Sweller interview](https://www.teachermagazine.com/au_en/articles/cognitive-load-theory-teaching-strategies)) argue for setting parameters from a readiness diagnostic; Tomlinson's model supports differentiating content, process and product by readiness and interest, though its "learning profile" strand overlaps the discredited learning-styles idea and should be ignored ([NSW on Tomlinson](https://education.nsw.gov.au/teaching-and-learning/professional-learning/teacher-quality-and-accreditation/strong-start-great-teachers/refining-practice/differentiating-learning/what-to-differentiate)). The verified age statements are few and specific: older students read praise after success as a low-ability signal while younger students read it as high ability, and **64 %** of adolescents prefer quiet private praise to loud public praise ([Hattie & Timperley 2007](https://www.uky.edu/~gmswan3/575/Hattie_Timperly_2007.pdf)); random-selection gimmicks "work well with younger children, but may not work so well for older students" ([Wiliam keynote](https://www.dylanwiliam.org/Dylan_Wiliams_website/Papers_files/Cambridge%20AfL%20keynote.doc)); review programmes were strongest "especially in the elementary grades" while weekly quizzes helped "even at the secondary level" ([Rosenshine 2012](https://www.aft.org/sites/default/files/Rosenshine.pdf)); Chinese psychology texts assign 形象抽象 → 经验型抽象 → 理论型抽象 thinking to the three bands ([清华社 教育心理学](http://www.tup.tsinghua.edu.cn/upload/books/yz/071572-01.pdf)); a 说课 handout contrasts 初中 "感性认识阶段" with 高中 "专题形式…更注重学生的理性认识" ([陕西师大 说课简介](https://his.snnu.edu.cn/__local/C/88/76/867429B3AF5DD1B914C5E12036A_D35D8845_9400.doc)); and policy fixes 零起点 and 游戏化 for grade 1, no written homework for grades 1-2 and the 60/90-minute caps ([幼小衔接 2021](http://www.moe.gov.cn/srcsite/A06/s3327/202104/t20210408_525137.html); [教基厅函〔2021〕13号](http://www.moe.gov.cn/srcsite/A06/s3321/202104/t20210425_528077.html)). The competition and licensing anchors tell the agent which grades judges expect it to handle natively: NTCE 小学 sample tasks are grade 3-4 (《荷花》, 分一分,《题西林壁》,《三角形的特性》) ([小学面试大纲 repost](https://img3.233.com/2019-11/21/157430425098861.doc)), and the 华容 competition pinned material to 五年级、八年级、高二 ([华容县通知](https://huarong.gov.cn/uploadfiles/201903/20190313153918233.docx)). Subjects change the opening, the board and the unit of a section more than the loop. In 语文 the unit is a text block and the moves are 朗读 and 表演: characters learned only inside the sentence (斯霞), the teacher's 范读 followed by the child's echo (于永正), one theme word linking texts (窦桂梅), one 字族/词串/句群 block per lesson (薛法根), 2-4 板块 each with one 主问题 and full pupil activity (余映潮), and at secondary level "用语文的方法教语文" and 教读 versus 自读 weighting (程翔); NTCE 语文 samples emphasise 朗读、意境、生活联系 ([小学面试大纲 repost](https://img3.233.com/2019-11/21/157430425098861.doc)). In 数学 the unit is a problem and the moves are conflict, variation and error: a situation in which the old tool fails (张兴华), a stem sentence repeated across contexts with concrete and abstract on the same board line (Shanghai), 变式 that changes one element and holds the rest constant ([NCETM Five Big Ideas](https://www.ncetm.org.uk/teaching-for-mastery/mastery-explained/five-big-ideas-in-teaching-for-mastery/)), 一题多解 → 多解归一 (孙维刚), 化错 (华应龙), and at 高中 "推理是数学的'命根子'" with the pupil posing the problem (章建跃). In 英语 the verified lineage is 情境 → text content → 阅读圈 roles (王咏梅), with 3P (Presentation / Practice / Production) inside 新授 ([亿图 template](https://mm.edrawsoft.cn/template/193411), secondary) and a board split into 单词/句型/例句 with a scratch column on the right ([杨艳、杨六兰 2012](https://xbsk.xcc.edu.cn/xcxyxbsk/article/pdf/20120146)). In 物理 and 化学 the opening is an experiment or one prop that yields two phenomena (顾雨薇), pupils must handle the apparatus themselves (楚得群), and the section ends in a 小结检测 (竹山). In 历史 and 道德与法治 the board favours 年表式、地理方位式、对比式 ([陕西师大 说课简介](https://his.snnu.edu.cn/__local/C/88/76/867429B3AF5DD1B914C5E12036A_D35D8845_9400.doc)), a section must keep a named person, a plot and a motive with first-hand evidence (李惠军), and units are built as 回顾—集思—解决—提升 with a 行为体验 writing task (袁小梅) or a 课前三分钟时政播报 habit ([2019 楷模候选人简介](https://m.gmw.cn/baijia/2019-07/18/33007508.html)).

Put together, the agent's per-section template is the reconciled loop with the numbers attached: 定向 — the 课题 and one to three goals on the 正板书 inside the first minute, one live hook the student recognises; 尝试 — a bounded attempt (2-3 minutes, *proposed target*) on the section's item, or a full worked example first when the diagnostic shows a missing prerequisite; 后教 — no more than 5-8 minutes of continuous input (*cited* Shanghai bursts of "5 minutes or so"; 杜郎口 6-8-minute links), each idea in pre-scripted short sentences, the student's own attempt written on the board before the correct version, at least two examples plus one non-example; 检查 — one hinge-style question with a 3-5-second silence, the five 助答 moves in order on a miss, No Opt Out until the student states the answer, then one 追问 or Stretch It; 练习 — one exercise answered in writing, corrected in-lesson, a 变式 of the taught case, with the 80 % success rate as the difficulty dial; 自结 — the student says the rule back, the agent reads the 小结 off the board, and the exit ticket's error points seed the next lesson's 5-8-minute opening review. The feedback line is task, process and self-regulation with no person-praise, and the board obeys the 正/副板书 split: title plus one line per section header and at most two key lines each that are never erased, and a scratch block for the current exercise that is cleared when the section ends.

## Conclusion

The most useful thing this research changes is the framing of "Chinese versus international" pedagogy. Read at the level of moves, the two traditions are not competing philosophies but the same loop described by different instruments: the Chinese sources fix the artefacts and the time budget (教案 with 依据, 正板书, 说课 rationale, ≤10 minutes of talk, the last 15 minutes for the pupil, 堂堂清), the international sources fix the dials and the vetoes (80 % success, ≥3-second waits, comment-only feedback, no discovery-first on new content, no person-praise, no learning styles). The one apparent conflict — 先学后教 against the Sutton Trust's veto on discovery — resolves once 先学 is treated as a bounded diagnostic attempt followed by mandatory teacher-led explanation and comparison, which is precisely what Japanese neriage, 洋思's 后教 and Rosenshine's worked-example sequence all do. That reframing gives the agent a single lesson workflow with two explicit branches (worked example first, or attempt first) chosen per section by a readiness diagnostic rather than by age, and it gives the knowledge base a shape: a 教案/说课 schema, a questioning loop with numbers, a three-level feedback template, board rules, a per-band register table, per-subject opening and board conventions, and a veto list drawn from both the Sutton Trust and the masters' own anti-patterns.

Three implications follow. First, the agent can be evaluated as a 青教赛 contestant: the rubric rows that recur across the opened 评分表 (目标清晰具体, 重难点明确有依据, 流程循序渐进, 讲授精当富有启发性, 学生参与充分, 时间动态调控, 板书提纲挈领无错字, 达成度高) are checkable in a transcript and a board snapshot, and weighting the process block at about half mirrors the judges. Second, the evidence hierarchy should be carried into the knowledge base as metadata — Rosenshine, Black and Wiliam and Hattie and Timperley are primary and experimentally backed; Shanghai and Japanese practice are documented but the one controlled transfer study (the DfE evaluation) found no attainment effect; the Chinese masters' effect figures are press or school claims; Hattie's aggregate rankings are contested; and a named set of items (王道俊 第七版, 魏书生's step contents, the national 青教赛 weights, the 小学/中学 NTCE table, the full TLAC list, 一师一优课 indicators) remains unverified — so that a rule's confidence, not just its text, is available at generation time. Third, only two of the cited numbers are cheap to measure locally from the first pilot — the per-exercise success rate and the silence held after each question — and a third proxy, "did the student speak or write in the last 90 seconds" (孙双金's 四小 translated to video), is worth logging alongside them, because the masters' and the researchers' rules agree that the lesson is going wrong long before the exit ticket says so.
