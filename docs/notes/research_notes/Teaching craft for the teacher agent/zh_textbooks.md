Labelling convention used throughout: **[P]** = textbook / syllabus / policy prescription; **[R]** = research evidence or measured data; **[I]** = my own inference; **[NV]** = not verified against a fetched source (commonly repeated in Chinese pedagogy but I could not open a primary page). Chinese terms and short verbatim quotes are kept. Numbers are labelled as cited unless stated otherwise.

Method note: web search was US-routed and Chinese search engines / Baidu Baike blocked automated access, so most evidence comes from documents I downloaded and text-extracted directly (exam syllabi PDFs, university course outlines, journal PDFs, press articles). ## Which textbooks are actually used in 师范 programmes and 教师资格证 preparation?

### Takeaway
The canonical pair is 王道俊、郭文安《教育学》(人民教育出版社) for pedagogy and 陈琦、刘儒德《教育心理学》(高等教育出版社; the sister volume《当代教育心理学》北京师范大学出版社) for educational psychology; both are named as the reference books of the national 教育硕士 333《教育综合》syllabus. 教师资格证 candidates mostly study commercial exam-prep textbooks keyed to the 教育部 2011《考试标准》and the NTCE 考试大纲, whose content (教学原则、教学方法、教学基本环节、教案、教学技能) is a condensed version of the same textbooks.

## What lesson structure and "上课的基本要求" do the textbooks prescribe?

### Takeaway
The textbooks prescribe five 教学工作基本环节 (备课、上课、课外作业、课外辅导、成绩检查评定) and, inside a lesson, the sequence 导入 → 新课讲授 → 巩固练习/提问 → 课堂总结 → 板书/作业; a good lesson is judged by eight criteria (目的明确、内容正确、方法得当、结构合理、语言艺术、气氛热烈、板书有序、态度从容). Reform models converge on a hard time rule: the teacher talks for at most 10-15 minutes and at least 15-30 minutes of the 45 go to student practice done and corrected in class.

## What are the classic 教学原则 and the 《学记》 sources, with their operational meaning?

### Takeaway
The exam-standard list is eight principles each with 2-4 "贯彻要求"; the classical 《学记》 lines (道而弗牵、强而弗抑、开而弗达; 长善救失; 教学相长; 豫时孙摩; 善问如攻坚木; 善待问如撞钟) are taught as the ancestors of 启发性、因材施教、循序渐进 and of questioning technique.

### Cited Findings
- [P] 333 syllabus list: "科学性和思想性统一、理论联系实际、直观性、启发性、循序渐进、巩固性、发展性、因材施教等教学原则的涵义和要求" — [S1](http://cdn1.kybimg.com/ohr/2019/07/13/094104_5d2936b01d8fa.pdf).
- [P] Principles with 贯彻要求 (verbatim, exam-prep condensation of the textbook): 
  - 思想性（教育性）和科学性相统一: "①教师要保证教学的科学性；②发掘教材的思想性…③要重视补充有价值的资料、事例或录像；④教师不断提高自己的专业水平和思想修养".
  - 直观性: "①正确选择直观教具和现代化教学手段；②直观要与讲解相结合；③重视运用语言直观".
  - 启发性: "①调动学生学习的主动性。②启发学生独立思考，发展学生的逻辑思维能力；③让学生动手，培养独立解决问题的能力；④发扬教学民主".
  - 巩固性: "①在理解的基础上巩固；②重视组织各种复习；③在扩充改组和运用知识中积极巩固".
  - 循序渐进（系统性）: "①按教材的系统性进行教学；②注意主要矛盾，解决好重点与难点的教学；③由浅入深，由易到难，由简到繁".
  - 因材施教: "①针对学生的特点进行有区别的教学；②采取有效措施，使有才能的学生得到充分的发展".
  - 理论联系实际: "①书本知识的教学要注重联系实际；②重视培养学生运用知识的能力；③正确处理知识教学与技能训练的关系；④补充必要的乡土教材".

- [P] 夸美纽斯《大教学论》 "首次提出并论证了直观性、系统性、量力性、巩固性和自觉性等一系列教学方法"; 赞科夫 developmental principles "高难度、高速度、理论知识起主导作用、理解学习过程、使所有学生包括差生都得到发展"; 布鲁纳 teaching principles "动机/结构/程序/强化" — [S2](https://img3.233.com/2019-07/26/156412076595801.pdf).

### Inferences
- [I] Operationally, 启发性 + 学记 imply an explanation should end in a question the student can answer with a "jump" (跳一跳), not in the conclusion; 巩固性 implies every new point is followed by an application before moving on; 循序渐进 + 量力性 imply ordering board points 由浅入深 and pitching at the 最近发展区.

- The 王道俊/郭文安 wording of each principle's 贯彻要求 was not read from the book itself; [S2] is an exam-prep condensation and the phrasing is identical across prep sites, so it is likely faithful.

## What do the 教学技能 (microteaching) textbooks say about each skill?

### Takeaway

### Inferences
- [I] For a one-student live lesson, the "叫答" step collapses, so the questioning loop is: pose (one clear question, at the 重点/难点/衔接处) → wait 3-5 s (10-15 s if open) → student answers → wait 3-5 s → 理答 (repeat/clarify, split, change angle, hint, complete) → evaluate specifically and proportionally → 追问 at 易错点.

## What do the textbooks say about 备课 (教案) and 说课?

### Takeaway
备课 = 三备 (备教材、备学生、备教法) producing 学期/单元/课时 plans; an 教案 has 目标 (三维 or 核心素养), 重难点, 教学过程 (导入/新授/练习/小结), 板书设计, 作业, and (post-lesson) 教学反思; 说课 adds the reasoning (教材分析、学情分析、教法、学法、程序、板书) and is judged on theory-backed justification.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- [I] A lesson-generation prompt should require, before the sections: (a) one-line 学情 statement (what the student already knows / typical misconception), (b) 1-2 重点 and 1 难点, (c) three-part goals, (d) the 板书 outline, (e) 作业 with tiers; and after the lesson: a 反思 note. ## What do the textbooks say about differentiation by age/grade, 练习设计, and 错误处理?

### Takeaway
Textbooks tie grade bands to thinking type (小学 = 具体形象→抽象过渡 with a 关键年龄 around grade 4; 初中 = 经验型抽象逻辑; 高中 = 理论型抽象逻辑), require teaching at the 最近发展区 under the 量力性原则, and prescribe 因材施教 as 有区别的教学; practice must be 当堂、及时订正, 变式 rather than repetition, tiered (分层、弹性、个性化, with legal time caps), and errors are to be diagnosed at the 易错点 and corrected by the student with positive, specific feedback.

### Cited Findings
- [P] Age stages and thinking: "童年期(6、7岁～11、12岁)，相当于学龄初期；少年期(11、12岁～14、15岁)，相当于学龄中期；青年期(14、15岁～25岁)"; "出生至三岁，主要是直观行动思维；三岁至六七岁(学前期)，主要是具体形象思维；六七岁至十一二岁(学龄初期)，主要是形象抽象思维；十一二岁至十四五岁(少年期)，主要是以经验型为主的抽象逻辑思维；十四五岁至十七八岁(青年初期)，主要是以理论型为主的抽象逻辑思维"; teaching implication "教学内容和教学策略的选择都要考虑受教育者的发展水平和年龄特点，要遵循量力性原则…抓好各个阶段间的联系与过渡…在注意共同性的同时又要照顾个别差异"; "教育要适应'最近发展区'，走在发展的前面"; 关键期 "一旦错过就难以弥补的特定阶段" — [S28](http://www.tup.tsinghua.edu.cn/upload/books/yz/071572-01.pdf).

### Inferences
- [I] Grade-band translation: 小学低年级 (1-2): concrete objects/pictures/stories, one idea per section, very short board lines, no written homework (法定); 中年级 (3-4): the transition band — pair every abstract statement with a concrete case, start explicit 学法 (how to check your own work); 高年级 (5-6): begin symbolic/abstract summaries and 变式 problems; 初中: 经验型 abstract reasoning — argue from examples to rules, 追问 "为什么"; 高中: 理论型 — deductive structure, student states the rule and proves/justifies it, more autonomy.
- [I] "错误资源化" (treating errors as resources) is not textbook wording in what I fetched; the verified lineage is 学记 长善救失 → 顾泠沅 "生成资源" and "从误解处提高效率" → 洋思 后教 focused on 先学 errors.

## Influential Chinese pedagogical traditions taught in these books

### Takeaway
All the celebrated Chinese models are variations on one skeleton — set the goal, let the student try/self-study first, teach only what the attempt exposed, practise and correct in the same lesson, summarise — with differing time rules; the two theoretical anchors are 陶行知's 教学做合一 (teach the way the thing is done) and 苏霍姆林斯基's insistence that there is no abstract student.

### Inferences
- [I] Common skeleton (verified across 邱学华 [S13/S14], 洋思 [S16/S17], 卢仲衡 [S21], 杜郎口 [S18]): (1) state the goal in ≤1-2 min; (2) student attempts/self-studies with a guiding question; (3) teacher teaches only what the attempt exposed, briefly; (4) practise again in-lesson, independently, and correct immediately; (5) summarise common errors and systematise. For a one-student AI lesson, "兵教兵" becomes "student explains it back / teaches the tutor".

## Operational rules for an AI teacher

Each rule is one line, testable, with source ids. [P] = prescribed by a cited textbook/syllabus/model; [I] = inference from cited material. Lesson = 3-5 sections, each with board points, spoken lines and an exercise, for one student.

### Whole lesson (structure and time)
1. Open by writing the 课题 and stating 1-3 concrete goals (知识/方法/态度) within the first minute; every later section must reference one of them (回应式结尾 closes the loop) — [P] S2, S13 rule 1, S16, S34.
2. Order sections 由浅入深、由易到难、由简到繁; no section may require a concept not yet taught or reviewed (循序渐进/量力性) — [P] S2, S23.
3. Budget teacher talk at ≤10 minutes per 45-minute equivalent (≈ ≤25% of lesson time); give ≥50% of time to student attempts, practice and answering — [P] S13 rule 8, S17, S21.
4. Keep segments short: no explanation block longer than 6-8 minutes without a student action (answer, try, write) — [P] S18; [I].
5. Each section = 导入/衔接 → 讲解 (one idea) → 检查 (one question) → 练习 (one exercise) → mini-summary; the lesson ends with 当堂训练 (independent) + 小结 + 作业 — [P] S2, S16, S21.
6. State 1-2 重点 and exactly 1 难点 in the plan; the 难点 gets the most 变式 examples and the 追问 — [P] S2, S8.
7. Prefer "先试后导": before explaining a new rule, pose a 尝试题 the student can partly do with prior knowledge, then teach from what the attempt exposed — [P] S13, S14, S16.
8. Content per lesson is small: "内容不要太多"; one well-worked problem beats ten sloppy ones — [P] S13 rule 11, S19.

### 导入 (opening)
9. Choose one 导入 type from {直接, 温故, 情境/故事, 问题/悬念, 练习/实验} and keep it ≤2 minutes; a 温故 opening must name the specific old knowledge that the new knowledge builds on (以旧引新) — [P] S2, S9, S15.
10. The opening must both 引起注意 and 揭示课题; do not open with an unrelated story — [P] S2, S9.

### 讲解 (explanation)
11. Explain from the student's existing knowledge using 观察、类比、归纳 and one concrete case before the abstract statement; then put the new concept into the existing system (纳入知识体系) — [P] S19, S2 (直观性: 直观要与讲解相结合).
12. Spoken lines must be 准确、简练、生动, 概念准确、思路清晰; use 普通话-standard wording and standard terms; no jargon the grade has not met — [P] S10, S8.
13. Do not deliver the conclusion the student can reach with a 跳一跳 question (开而弗达); end each explanation with a question, not a summary — [P] S11, S12, S19.
14. Pair every abstract rule with its 易错点 ("分母不能为零"-style caution) and one contrasting non-example — [P] S19; [I].
15. Avoid filler questioning (small-step fill-in-the-blank "对不对?"); each question must require thinking at 理解 level or above — [P] S19, S26.

### 提问 and checking understanding
16. Use 2-3 core questions per lesson, placed at the 重点、难点 and 新旧知识衔接处, phrased in one clear sentence with only the needed terms — [P] S26.
17. After asking, wait 3-5 s (10-15 s for open questions) before speaking; after the student answers, wait 3-5 s before responding — [P] S26.
18. If the answer is wrong or absent, use in order: 重复发问/申明题意 → 分解难点 → 转换角度 → 适当提示 → 补充修正; never give the answer on the first miss — [P] S26.
19. After a correct answer, 追问 "为什么/怎么知道" at least once at the 难点 (探索性追问), then move on — [P] S26.
20. Feedback must match answer quality and be specific (name what was right and what was missing); praise effort for weak but serious answers; never "一棒子打死" — [P] S26, S2 (疏导原则).
21. Check understanding by having the student restate or teach back the rule in their own words before the exercise (学生讲, 兵教兵 analogue) — [P] S17, S22; [I].

### 练习 (exercise design)
22. Every section's exercise follows immediately and is corrected in-lesson ("练在当堂、订正在当堂"); no exercise is left unmarked — [P] S13 rule 9, S16, S27.
23. Final 当堂训练 has two rounds: 模仿练习 (may look back at the example) then 达标检测 (independent, timed, no hints) — [P] S16.
24. Exercises are 变式 of the taught case (change surface, keep structure), not repeats; include one that transfers to a new situation (迁移点) — [P] S21, S19, S23.
25. Offer tiers: basic (must), raised (should), extension (may); harder/more for strong students, easier/fewer for struggling ones — [P] S13 rule 12, S27.
26. Homework must respect legal limits: none written for 小学一二年级; total ≤60 min/day 小学三至六年级, ≤90 min/day 初中 (so a single lesson's assignment should be a small fraction of that) — [P] S27.
27. Homework follows 目的性、针对性、趣味性、层次性、多样性、开放性 and is 全批全改 with specific feedback — [P] S2, S27.

### 板书 (text board)
28. Use 提纲式 as default: title + 3-5 numbered points + key formula/term; add a 副板 area for the current worked example and erase it between examples — [P] S2; [I] (主/副板 [NV]).
29. Board text is 言简意赅、条理性强: ≤12 characters per point, same order as the spoken explanation, written as each point is taught (同步), never a wall of text — [P] S8, S10.
30. At lesson end the board must read as a complete, correct summary of the lesson (可回顾); the 小结 walks through it — [P] S2, S8.

### 反馈 and error handling
31. Diagnose before re-teaching: identify whether the error is a 记忆 slip, a 理解 gap, or an 易错点 misconception; re-teach only the missing piece (后教 targets 先学 errors) — [P] S16, S17, S19.
32. Make the student correct their own work (自行修正/自检) and state the reason for the error; teacher adds the 共性错误 rule in the 小结 — [P] S10, S21.
33. Use errors as material: when a wrong method yields a different-looking answer, ask "为什么不一样?" and verify together (旧知中引发冲突) — [P] S19.
34. Keep a "堂堂清" rule: unmastered goal → immediate remediation exercise in the same lesson; still unmastered → carried to the next lesson's 导入 — [P] S16, S31.
35. Attribute success to effort and method, not luck or fixed ability (维纳 归因; 自我效能); reinforce with success experiences to build "成功—兴趣—成功" — [P] S2, S26.

### 小结 and closing
36. Close with (a) 归纳 of the board points, (b) 回应 to the opening goals, and optionally (c) one 发散/悬念 question for next time — [P] S2, S34, S3.
37. Ask the student to give the summary first (自结); the teacher completes it — [P] S22 (自结), S21 (结 by teacher: 概括全貌、纠正共性错误).

### Grade-band rules
38. 小学低年级 (1-2): concrete objects/pictures/story situations for every idea; one idea per section; frequent short actions (say/point/write) every 2-3 minutes; questions at 回忆/理解 level with immediate praise; no written homework — [P] S28, S29, S25, S27; [I] on cadence.
39. 小学中年级 (3-4): the 关键年龄 transition — every abstract statement must be paired with a worked concrete case and a picture/diagram on the board; start teaching 学法 (how to check an answer); introduce simple 变式 — [P] S30, S28, S21; [I].
40. 小学高年级 (5-6): symbolic summaries and rules allowed after a concrete case; two-tier exercises; 追问 "为什么"; begin self-checking (自检) routines — [P] S28, S21; [I].
41. 初中 (7-9): reason from examples to rules (经验型抽象逻辑); make the student state and justify the rule; use cognitive-conflict examples and 类比迁移 with explicit 易错点; more independent 当堂训练; watch emotional volatility — feedback stays specific and calm — [P] S28, S29, S19, S16.
42. 高中 (10-12): deductive structure (definition → theorem/rule → proof/justification → application); let the student choose the approach and defend it; higher proportion of 分析/综合/评价 questions; longer independent work blocks, autonomy in goal-setting — [P] S28, S29, S26, S3 (高中 探究性教学).
