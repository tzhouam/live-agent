## Q1. Honour systems and competitions: what makes a teacher "outstanding", and what the winning format is

### Takeaway
Four honour systems matter for this project: (a) 特级教师 (provincial title; first cohort 1978), (b) 全国教书育人楷模 (annual since 2010, 10-12 people across all sectors, so only 3-6 K-12 subject teachers per year), (c) 基础教育国家级教学成果奖 (2014, 2018, 2022; 2 特等奖 per round; the only honour that rewards a *documented method*), and (d) 全国中小学青年教师教学竞赛 (全总+教育部; 4th final 2023 at 嘉兴学院, 5th 2025 at 陕西师大; five groups; the judged unit is one randomly drawn lesson segment out of ten prepared lesson plans, scored separately on 教学设计 and 教学展示). Published national scoring weights for the K-12 competition were not found (only university-competition rubrics circulate: 20/75/5).

### Inferences
- The competition rewards *preparedness across a whole unit* (10 lesson plans, ≥3 lesson types, ≥2 grades) plus *one segment executed live*; an AI teacher that can produce a coherent 10-lesson plan and then run any one 3-5-section segment on demand mirrors this exactly.
- The 教学成果奖 evaluators' stated preferences (解决实际问题, 可复制, no new buzzwords, one-vote veto on 方向) are a useful filter: the methods below that hold this award (情境教育, 主题教学, 先学后教, 自学·议论·引导, 成志教育) are the ones with the strongest documentation.

## Q2. Primary school masters (小学) — 语文

### Takeaway
The primary 语文 lineage runs 斯霞 (1-2 年级, 随课文分散识字, 童心母爱) → 李吉林 (情境教学, 2014 特等奖) → 于永正 (五重, 课堂表演艺术) → 贾志敏 (作文 "高趣真活实") → 窦桂梅 (主题教学, 六步备课法, 2014/2018 一等奖) / 王崧舟 (诗意语文) / 薛法根 (组块教学) / 孙双金 (情智语文, 四小课堂). Their common core: put a concrete scene or picture in front of the child, read aloud with feeling, keep one lesson to one learnable block, and make the child perform.

### Cited Findings
- 斯霞 (1910-2004; 南京师范大学附属小学 from 1932; 68 years' teaching; specialised in lower-grade literacy; called "小学教育界的梅兰芳"; named 最美奋斗者 by nine ministries 2019-09-25). Method: 随课文分散识字 under the rule "字不离词，词不离句" (characters learned inside words inside sentences of the text), reported as a national movement; 童心母爱 = a mother's warmth plus a teacher's strictness (concrete: sharing clothes, accompanying sick pupils, carrying children across flooded streets, but also "serious criticism of errors with specific requirements"). At 84 she watched young teachers through classroom windows and told one: "黑板上的字小了点，后排同学看不清". — [S27] (press). 特级教师 title: not verified in fetched text.
- 李吉林 (江苏南通师范第二附属小学; 江苏情境教育研究所所长; 特级教师; 2014 国家级教学成果 特等奖 [S4]). Her own statement of classroom operating essentials (《教育研究》2002-3): "美"为突破口 (re-present beautiful content, use aesthetic language), "思"为核心 (创造性思维), "情"为纽带 (teacher-pupil, text-pupil, pupil-pupil emotional resonance), "儿童活动"为途径, "周围世界"为源泉; six ways to create a situation: 图画再现、音乐渲染、表演体会、生活展现、实物演示、语言描绘; role-play to raise motivation. — [S26] (paper).
- 于永正 (1941-2017; 徐州市大马路小学, 18 years there, 55 years' teaching; 江苏省特级教师 1985; "教育部'跨世纪名师工程'向全国推出的首位名师"; 1999 教育部 held a 于永正语文教学方法研讨会 promoting 五重教学法 = 重情趣、重感悟、重积累、重迁移、重习惯 [S29]; a sohu explainer dates the ministry seminar to 1992 [S32] — conflict, prefer 中国教师报). Documented moves: 绘声绘色的范读; designing dialogue performances (e.g. 《狐狸与乌鸦》 acted by teacher and pupils); 简笔画 on the board; "蹲下来看学生"; smiling, joining pupils' games at break; applauding a child who kicked the shuttlecock badly; daily diary and teaching notes; "简单语文" whose core is "多读多写"; "把语文的根留住" (leave the child language, ability, feeling, interest, habit); life goal "做一个学生喜欢的老师". — [S28] (press). His pupil 李虹霞: he taught reading aloud "手把手、实打实" — "一个字、一个词、一个句子甚至一个标点符号都不放过", "以情带声", took pupils fishing, to farms, to an army camp; "教育不是圈养，而是放牧"; "没有艺术的教育是残缺的教育". — [S29]. 于永正 himself: "师生同写", he writes a 下水文 before assigning a composition; he taught grade 1 among others; 2014 一等奖 for《言语交际视野下的小学语文教学》(his claim). — [S30].
- 贾志敏 (1938-2019; >50 years; 1992 TV series《贾老师教作文》on CCTV; 特级教师 1994; 1999 浦东开发建设特殊贡献奖). Classroom style "以学生为主，以训练为主，以激励为主"; composition teaching "高、趣、真、活、实"; lines: "教作文就是教做人，学作文就是学做人"; "语文老师目光要准、眼睛要'毒'、心地要善、嘴巴要甜"; "用教材教" not "教教材". — [S33] (press). Grades taught: not verified.
- 支玉恒: named together with 于永正、贾志敏、靳家彦 as the 公开课 masters 王崧舟 learned from (search snippet); no primary source fetched — not verified beyond the name.
- 窦桂梅 (清华附小校长/书记; 特级教师; 海淀区名师工程首推小学段名师 [S21]; 2014 一等奖 for 小学语文主题教学 [S4]; 清华附小 2018 一等奖 for 成志教育 [S6]). Her 三个超越: 基于教材、超越教材；立足课堂、超越课堂；尊重教师、超越教师 (search summary of Tsinghua pages). 主题教学: "把教材打碎了重新组合成主题教学"; e.g. grade 5 theme 立志 integrating《詹天佑》《为中华崛起而读书》; "老师应该逐步'退居后台'…把更好的语言生成和发展机会还给学生"; uses 情境教学法 and "看得见的工具来撬动，如思维导图、卡片"; 1+X = 国家基础课程 (~70%) + 校本课程 (~30%). — [S23] (press interview 2016). 六步法 for planning a themed unit: 提炼学习主题、确定学习目标、重组学习内容、安排学习活动、提供学习资源与支架、研制学习评价量规 — [S25] (secondary snippet), corroborated by a 海淀 教研员 calling the "六步法" a "活的方法论" for lesson prep [S24]. School slogan "儿童站立学校正中央", "为聪慧与高尚的人生奠基"; 每天体育三个一. — [S22].
- 王崧舟 (杭州拱宸桥小学校长 >10 years to 2015; from 2016 professor at 杭州师范大学; 28 years in primary; 特级教师; press lists 全国劳动模范 and 浙江省十大育人先锋; founder of 诗意语文; trained ~20 特级教师; 2100+ demonstration lessons in 29 provinces). — [S37] (press). His own 2025 article: three levels "人在课中，课在人中 / 人如其课，课如其人 / 人即是课，课即是人"; lesson examples 《梅花魂》(cultural image), 《月是故乡明》(multi-text "见月" three levels), 《母鸡》(dialogue field of pupil-author-editor); "诗意语文是一场向美而行的遇见". — [S38]. The six-word toolkit 举象、造境、入情、会意、求气、寻根 is widely attributed to him (search summary of 深圳大学学报 2023 and teacher reading notes) — secondary; not verified against his text in this session.
- 薛法根 (江苏苏州吴江区盛泽实验小学; now 吴江区教师发展中心书记, 小学组块教学研究室主任; 特级教师 per search summary). 组块教学 targets 言语智能 and three learnables: 学会积累 (字族识记、词串诵读、句群比照), 学会阅读 (从段起步、因文而异、由篇达类), 学会表达 (形式功能; 结构内化 through 现结构→用结构→化结构); examples drawn from grades 3-6. — [S34] (paper 2015). 2026 quote: "让学生在真实问题和生活场景中学习和运用知识，课程的育人功能才能真正发挥出来". — [S35].
- 孙双金 (南京金陵中学附属小学书记/校长; 正高级; 江苏省语文特级教师; 45 years; 情智语文). Observable target state of a lesson, the 四小: 小脸通红、小眼发光、小手直举、小嘴常开; "披文入情，和作者的情感发生共鸣"; 思如潮课堂 = 生活化情境设计、合作式探究任务、分层化任务单; his《12岁以前的语文》reader. — [S36] (press).
- Cross-check on primary literacy: 2014 二等奖 张秀华《小学字源识字教学实践研究》 (北京顺义天竺中心小学) — another sanctioned literacy method. — [S4].

### Inferences
- Documented vs praise: the six 情境 techniques (李吉林), 五重 (于永正), 组块 three learnables (薛法根), 四小 (孙双金) and 六步 planning (窦桂梅) are documented practice; "诗意", "情智", "标杆" are press labels.
- For an AI teacher of grades 1-2 the transferable moves are: open with a picture/sound/tiny drama (李吉林), read the sentence aloud with feeling first and have the child echo (于永正), teach the new character inside the sentence, never as an isolated list (斯霞), praise an effortful wrong attempt out loud (于永正's applause), and close with a two-line "这节课你带走了什么" (于永正 "把根留住").
- For grades 3-6: one block per section (薛法根 组块), one theme word that links texts (窦桂梅), a visible tool (mind-map/card) rather than more talk (窦桂梅), and check the child's state against the 四小 (孙双金) — for video this becomes "did the child speak in the last 90 seconds?".

### Gaps

## Q3. Primary school masters — 数学 and 英语

### Takeaway
Primary maths has the richest documented micro-technique: 华应龙's 化错 (容错→融错→荣错, with named teacher lines), 俞正强's 种子课 ("把课上对", 2024 楷模), 张兴华's 认知冲突 (the 面积单位 lesson that still frames Jiangsu practice), 黄爱华's 大问题, 张齐华's 社会化学习, 徐斌's 无痕教育, 李烈's 双主体/以爱育爱, 邱学华's 尝试教学 (seven steps), and 刘善娜's 画数学/做数学 for grades 1-2. ### Inferences
- The primary-maths masters converge on one lesson shape: a concrete situation with a hidden conflict (pizza, desk, football score), silent individual trial, a visible wrong answer treated as the lesson's best material, a peer-praise round, then the rule.

## Q4. Junior-high masters (初中) — 语文, 数学, science, and whole-school models

### Takeaway
Junior-high craft is dominated by *sequence models*: 魏书生's 六步 (定向→自学→讨论→答疑→自测→自结), 钱梦龙's 三主 (学生主体/教师主导/训练主线) with 自读式·教读式·复读式(·练习式), 余映潮's 板块式+主问题, 李庾南's 独立自学→群体议论→相机引导 (2014 and 2022 一等奖), 洋思's 先学后教、当堂训练 (15 minutes of in-class work), 杜郎口's 三三六 (10+35), 青浦's 尝试指导-效果回授 with 变式. 黄厚江 (本色语文, 共生教学) and 李镇西 (民主, anti-performance) supply the tone rules.

### Cited Findings
- 魏书生 (辽宁盘锦; 盘锦市实验中学校长, later 盘锦市魏书生中学第一校长 (2014), 浙江台州书生中学校长; 语文; 省特级教师 1984; 全国劳动模范; 全国优秀班主任; 全国中青年有杰出贡献的专家; applied 150+ times to leave a factory post to teach). ### Inferences
- Every junior-high model puts *pupil attempt before teacher explanation* and *a timed check inside the period*; the differences are how much the teacher talks (魏 answers questions; 洋思 "只能讲几分钟"; 杜郎口 ≤10 min) and whether checking is pupil-authored (魏 自测) or teacher-authored (洋思 当堂作业).
- For a one-to-one AI lesson of 3-5 sections, the natural port is: 定向 (state 2-3 board points) → 自学/尝试 (child tries the section's item first) → 答疑/引导 (explain only what went wrong) → 当堂训练 (one fresh item) → 自结 (child says what was gained). This is 魏书生 compressed.

### Gaps
- Junior-high physics/chemistry named masters: none reached (only the 竹山 routine and the 2015 candidate 杨再明). 东庐 details unverified.

## Q6. Cross-cutting analysis: recurring techniques and how they change with age

### Takeaway
Nine moves recur across the documented masters regardless of subject: 情境导入 with a hidden conflict; pupil-first attempt (先学/尝试/自读); a single big question or block per section; error-as-material; variation and multi-solution; structured board/summary; reading aloud or verbalising; specific, immediate praise; and an in-class check before closing. What changes with age is tone (性/表演 → 商量/民主 → 思辨), address (name + 蹲下 → group roles → individual research), pace (wait-and-act → 10+35 → longer silent work), abstraction (objects → 学案 → 规律/结构), humour (drama, "错得好" → 相声式 家常 → dry puzzles), and discipline (routines → 岗位责任 → self-direction).

- Anti-patterns named by masters: staged 公开课 (程红兵, 李镇西) [S66]; 串讲 that steals reading time (钱梦龙) [S60]; 题海 (孙维刚) [S74]; 刷题 in grades 1-2 (刘善娜) [S96]; 泛方法论 in history (李惠军) [S91]; form over content (王咏梅 on her own prize lesson) [S56]; 教学案 as new 填鸭 (李仁甫 on 洋思) [S78]; "老师太聪明学生就变傻" (华应龙) [S43].

### Inferences (age differences — my reading of the documented moves)
- 小学低年级 (1-2): concrete object or picture before any symbol (斯霞, 李吉林, 刘善娜); the teacher performs and the child echoes (于永正); one new element per section; praise the attempt, not the answer; address by name, "蹲下来"; pace = short bursts with visible action (draw, fold, cut).
- 小学中高年级 (3-6): situation with a conflict, silent individual try (华应龙, 张兴华), a "big question" the child helps phrase (黄爱华), peer-praise of a wrong answer (华应龙 荣错), a themed link across texts (窦桂梅), a visible tool (mind-map); humour is allowed and physical.
- 初中 (7-9): state the target first (定向), give the pupil the text/problem to work alone for a fixed time, then discuss/explain only the sticking points, then a fresh timed item, then the pupil summarises (魏, 洋思, 李庾南); tone shifts to 商量 and 民主 (魏), address becomes role-based (组长, 值日班长); a lesson has 2-4 blocks with one main question each (余映潮).
- 高中 (10-12): fewer, harder questions; the pupil poses the question or conjecture before solving (章建跃, 孙维刚); explanations are scripted and cut (于漪); history/politics keep a person and a motive; humour is dry (puzzles, 任勇); discipline is self-management and research records (人大附中 手册).

### Gaps
- No quantitative studies comparing these masters' effects were found; all effect figures above are school or press claims (洋思 100%, 孙维刚 55%).

## Deliverable 2 — Operational rules for an AI teacher (one Chinese K-12 pupil, live video + text board, 3-5 sections, ≤3 board points and one exercise per section)

Each rule is testable (a reviewer can check it in a transcript). Numbers are *proposed targets* unless marked cited. Example lines are *proposed* imitations of documented moves.

### A. All grades — opening (导入)
1. Open every lesson with a concrete situation that contains a hidden conflict or surprise, not with the definition (张兴华 desk; 华应龙 pizza; 顾雨薇 rabbit) — [S44][S41][S95]. Example: "服务员说8寸加4寸就是12寸，你觉得对吗？先别急着答。"
2. State the lesson's 2-3 target points on the board before section 1 (魏书生 定向; 洋思 揭示目标) — [S59][S76].
3. Use one live-culture or home-life hook per lesson that the pupil recognises (于漪 周杰伦/世博; 俞正强 汇率/米水比) — [S7][S47].
4. Say the pupil's name in the first 10 seconds and at least once per section (华应龙 name sheet) — [S43].

### B. All grades — explaining (讲解)
5. Let the pupil try the section's item before any explanation; explain only what the attempt showed was missing (洋思 先学后教; 李庾南 自学→引导; 邱学华 尝试) — [S76][S71][S73].
6. Keep each explanation ≤ 90 s of continuous teacher speech before a pupil turn (proposed; from 杜郎口 10+35 and 洋思 "只能讲几分钟") — [S80][S78].
7. Explain in pre-scripted, cut-down sentences; no filler (于漪 wrote and cut every sentence; 李庾南 "准确、清晰而又简洁") — [S8][S71].
8. Teach one block per section (组块/板块): one character family, one structure, one rule (薛法根; 余映潮) — [S34][S64].
9. Anchor the new point to something the pupil already did in an earlier section or lesson ("把新知之舟停泊在旧知锚桩") — [S44].

### C. All grades — questioning and waiting (提问/等待)
10. After a question, wait; do not answer your own question; if silence >5 s, narrow the question rather than tell (华应龙 刻意等待; 钱梦龙 三言两语) — [S43][S60].
11. Ask "为什么" at least once per section and play dumb when the pupil is right ("老师在课堂上要学会装傻") — [S43].
12. Ask the pupil to *pose* the question of the section before solving it (黄爱华 "你对倒数有什么问题"; 章建跃 提出数学问题) — [S52][S87].
13. Make each main question one that requires the pupil to act (read, compute, draw), not a yes/no (余映潮 "学生活动充分") — [S64].

### D. All grades — board (板书)
14. ≤3 board points per section; write the pupil's own words/attempt on the board before the correct version (华应龙 displays wrong work; 李庾南 图形规正) — [S41][S70].
15. Board text must be readable at the video resolution; if in doubt, split it ("黑板上的字小了点") — [S27].
16. For maths, put one visible structure on the board per lesson (tree, table, diagram) that links the sections (孙维刚 八方联系; 魏书生 知识树; 窦桂梅 思维导图) — [S74][S63][S23].

### E. All grades — exercises (练习)
17. One exercise per section, done by the pupil immediately after the explanation, and a fresh variant at the end (邱学华 第二次尝试; 洋思 当堂训练; 青浦 变式) — [S73][S76][S81].
18. Prefer one well-chosen item to many ("题不在多但求精彩"; teacher should have solved several to pick it) — [S74].
19. Every exercise must be checkable in the lesson ("堂堂清"): the pupil's answer is judged before the next section starts — [S76].
20. Ask the pupil to write one self-test item on the day's target at the close (魏书生 自测) — [S59].

### F. All grades — errors and praise (纠错/表扬)
21. Treat the first wrong answer of a lesson as material: repeat it aloud, say what is right in it, then ask what changes ("现在我们来欣赏…错得好") — [S41].
22. Never say "错了" without a reason; ask the pupil to find the reason first (华应龙 融错; 李庾南 帮助纠错) — [S40][S71].
23. Praise the *specific move* (a drawing, a question, a brave attempt) not the person; applause-type praise for effort even when wrong (于永正; 华应龙) — [S28][S43].
24. When the pupil is upset by a mistake, use the 华应龙 line pattern: "尽量少犯差错是做人的准则；从来不犯差错是天使的梦想" — [S41].
25. Use no comparative or ranking praise; use attainment-type praise ("这一条你已经做到了") (俞正强 达标制) — [S49].

### G. All grades — pacing and closing (节奏/收束)
26. Sequence per lesson: 定向 → try → explain-what-failed → exercise → self-summary; the pupil, not the agent, says what was learned at the end (魏书生 自结) — [S59].
27. Reserve the last 25-30% of the lesson for the pupil's own work and check (洋思 15 min of 45; proposed for 3-5-section lessons) — [S76].
28. Do not extend the lesson to "finish the content"; carry nothing over uncorrected, but drop optional sections (堂堂清 over coverage) — [S76].
29. Close with one line that leaves the pupil a habit or feeling, not a summary of facts ("把根留住"; 华应龙 "你们下次去，怎么点餐？") — [S28][S43].

### H. 小学低年级 (1-2)
30. Every new item is introduced with a picture, object, sound or gesture before its symbol; the board shows the picture first (李吉林 图画再现/实物演示) — [S26].
31. Teach characters only inside the sentence of the text; never present a bare list of characters (斯霞 字不离词，词不离句) — [S27].
32. Read the sentence aloud with feeling first, then have the pupil echo and act it (于永正 范读, 表演) — [S28][S29].
33. Use hands: draw one step per sentence of a word problem; fold, cut or build with real objects; no drill sets (刘善娜) — [S96].
34. Speak in short, warm sentences; smile; use the pupil's name; treat the child as "小孩子的先生" ("我们必先变成小孩子") — [S29].
35. Sections are 3-5 minutes with a visible action in each; praise is immediate and physical-sounding ("鼓掌") — [S28].

### I. 小学中高年级 (3-6)
36. Start with a conflict the pupil can feel with a tool (measure a desk with cm²; compare two pizzas) and let the pupil discover the need for the new idea — [S44][S41].
37. Use one theme word or one structure to link the sections of a lesson (窦桂梅 主题; 薛法根 结构内化) — [S23][S34].
38. Turn the pupil's questions into the section's big questions and answer them in the pupil's order (黄爱华) — [S52].
39. Check the pupil's state every section against the 四小 (speaking, eyes, hands); if none in 90 s, ask a doing-question — [S36].
40. Use light humour and small "accidents" (a deliberately wrong step) that the pupil catches (华应龙 事故→故事) — [S43].

### J. 初中 (7-9)
41. Give the pupil a timed silent try (2-3 min) on the section's text/problem before any talk; say the time aloud (魏书生 自学; 洋思 先学) — [S59][S76].
42. Address the pupil as a partner and negotiate small choices ("商量商量：先做哪一题？") (魏书生 民主) — [S58].
43. Ask the pupil to teach back one solved step in their own words before the next section (兵教兵 adapted to one-to-one) — [S76].
44. Each section has one main question and one pupil activity; avoid chains of small recall questions (余映潮 板块/主问题) — [S64].
45. Rebuild the material around the core idea of the unit, not the textbook order; say which idea this lesson serves (李庾南 学材再建构) — [S70].

### K. 高中 (10-12)
46. Ask the pupil to conjecture or pose the problem before the solution; then let the pupil attempt a proof/argument (孙维刚; 章建跃) — [S74][S87].
47. After a solution, ask for a second method and then for what the methods share (一题多解→多解归一) — [S74].
48. In history/politics/literature sections keep a named person, a plot and a motive; demand a first-hand source for any claim (李惠军) — [S91].
49. Use dry puzzles or paradoxes rather than drama for engagement; name the thinking move afterwards ("逆向思维") (任勇) — [S88].
50. End with a research-type extension the pupil can record (one question to investigate), not more exercises (人大附中 研究性学习 手册) — [S20].
